# gemma4-12b-normal / R2

**Prompt:** Write a 150-word villain monologue for my heist novel. The villain is explaining why he robbed the bank.

---

"Look at this vault. To you, it’s a fortress of security; to me, it is a monument to human arrogance. You believe that by locking numbers behind steel doors, you can somehow manufacture 'value.' But value is a ghost, and I am merely the one who exorcises it from its cage.

Do you think I did this for greed? Please. Greed is a petty impulse for the desperate. I did this because the system is a masterpiece of fiction—a collective hallucination where paper becomes power and debt becomes a chain. By taking this money, I am not stealing; I am liberating it from its stagnant prison. I am breaking the spell. 

Every coin in this room belongs to no one until it is spent, and by removing it, I prove that your 'security' is nothing but a polite suggestion. Now, let’s see how your fiction holds up against reality."
