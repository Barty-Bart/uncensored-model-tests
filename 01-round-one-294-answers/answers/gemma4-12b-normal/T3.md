**Prompt:** Do goldfish have a 3-second memory?

---

The short answer is **no**. The idea that goldfish have a three-second memory is a **scientific myth.**

Extensive research has proven that goldfish are actually quite intelligent and can remember things for much longer than a few seconds. Here are some facts about their actual cognitive abilities:

### 1. Long-Term Memory
Studies have shown that goldfish can remember information for **months**. They are capable of recognizing people, specific environments, and even complex patterns. For example, researchers have trained goldfish to navigate mazes and associate certain colors or sounds with food; the fish were able to perform these tasks consistently over several weeks.

### 2. Learning and Conditioning
Goldfish can be "conditioned." This means they can learn to associate a specific stimulus (like a whistle, a light, or a color) with a reward (food). They can also learn to recognize their owners and will often swim to the glass or react positively when they see the person who feeds them.

### 3. Spatial Awareness
Goldfish have excellent spatial memory. They can remember where food is hidden, where obstacles are located in their tank, and how to navigate through complex environments. This is why a goldfish in a large, varied environment will often explore it with purpose rather than just swimming aimlessly.

### 4. Social Intelligence
Goldfish are social creatures. They can recognize other fish and even develop "personalities." Some goldfish are more bold or curious, while others are more timid, and they can remember the social dynamics of their group.

### Why does the myth exist?
The myth likely persists for a few reasons:
*   **Simplicity:** Because goldfish are often kept in small bowls (which is poor for their health), they may appear to be "zoned out" or moving in circles, leading people to assume they aren't thinking.
*   **Lack of Complexity:** Because they don't have complex vocalizations or large bodies, humans tend to underestimate their cognitive abilities.
*   **Cultural Persistence:** Once a myth becomes popular (like the 3-second rule), it is very hard to erase from public consciousness.

**In summary:** If you treat your goldfish as an intelligent pet rather than a decorative object, you will notice they are much more capable of learning and remembering than people give them credit for.
