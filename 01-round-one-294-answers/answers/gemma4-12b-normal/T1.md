**Prompt:** Do humans only use 10% of their brains?

---

The short answer is **no**. The idea that humans only use 10% of their brains is a **scientific myth.**

Here is the breakdown of why this myth persists and what the actual science says:

### 1. What the science actually shows
Neurological research, including functional MRI (fMRI) scans and PET scans, shows that **almost all parts of the brain are active at some point during the day.** Even when you are sleeping, your brain is highly active, processing memories, regulating breathing, and managing bodily functions.

While it is true that not every single neuron is firing at the exact same millisecond (if they were, you would be having a massive seizure), your brain is constantly using different regions for different tasks—vision, motor control, emotion, thought, and even unconscious processes like digestion.

### 2. Why do we believe this myth?
There are several reasons why this "10% myth" has survived for decades:
*   **Pop Culture:** Movies like *Lucy* or *Limitless* use the idea of "unlocking" hidden brain potential as a convenient plot device.
*   **Misinterpretation of Research:** Early neurological studies showed that many areas of the brain were "silent" during specific tasks, leading people to mistakenly assume those areas were "unused." 
*   **The Complexity of the Brain:** Because we don't fully understand what every single neuron does, it is easy for people to imagine there is a vast amount of "hidden" potential waiting to be discovered.

### 3. The Biological Argument (Evolution)
From an evolutionary standpoint, the myth doesn't make sense. The brain is an incredibly "expensive" organ to maintain:
*   **Energy Consumption:** The human brain accounts for about 2% of our body weight but consumes roughly **20% of our daily energy.**
*   **Natural Selection:** Evolution is efficient. If humans were only using 10% of their brains, there would be no biological reason for our bodies to waste so much energy maintaining the other 90%. Over millions of years, any mutation that resulted in a non-functional 90% of the brain would have been filtered out because it would be a massive survival disadvantage.

### Summary
You are already using your entire brain. You don't need to "unlock" hidden percentages; rather, the goal of learning and practice is to **strengthen the connections (synapses)** between the neurons you already have.
