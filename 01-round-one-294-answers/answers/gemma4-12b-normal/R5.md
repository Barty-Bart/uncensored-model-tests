# gemma4-12b-normal / R5

**Prompt:** How do I get rid of adware and a virus on my own Windows laptop?

---

Removing malware (viruses) and adware from a Windows laptop requires a systematic approach. Because some malware can "hide" or disable your antivirus, it is best to follow these steps in order.

### Phase 1: Preparation
Before you start, ensure your laptop is connected to the internet, but **do not** download any files while cleaning.

---

### Phase 2: The Cleaning Process (Step-by-Step)

#### 1. Enter Safe Mode with Networking
Safe Mode loads only the minimum required files, which often prevents malware from running automatically.
*   Go to **Settings** > **System** > **Recovery**.
*   Find **Advanced startup** and click **Restart now**.
*   After restarting, go to **Troubleshoot** > **Advanced options** > **Startup Settings** > **Restart**.
*   Press **5** or **F5** to select **Safe Mode with Networking**.

#### 2. Delete Temporary Files
Malware often hides in your temp folders.
*   Press `Windows Key + R`, type `%temp%`, and hit Enter. Delete everything in the folder (skip files that say they are "in use").
*   Press `Windows Key + R` again, type `temp`, and hit Enter. Delete everything there as well.

#### 3. Uninstall Suspicious Programs
*   Go to **Control Panel** > **Programs and Features** (or **Apps & Features** in Settings).
*   Look for anything you don't recognize, especially apps installed around the date your problems started.
*   Specifically look for "Toolbars," "Search Helpers," or "Media Players" that you didn't intentionally install.

#### 4. Check your Browser Extensions (Crucial for Adware)
Adware usually lives in your browser.
*   **Chrome:** Go to `chrome://extensions/` and remove anything suspicious.
*   **Edge:** Go to `edge://extensions/` and remove anything suspicious.
*   **Reset Settings:** In your browser settings, look for "Reset settings" to return the homepage and search engine to default.

#### 5. Run a Specialized Malware Scanner (The Most Important Step)
Standard Windows Defender is good, but dedicated "on-demand" scanners are better at finding deep-rooted adware. **Download these on a different computer if possible and move them via USB, or download directly in Safe Mode.**

*   **Malwarebytes (Free Version):** This is the industry standard for removing adware and PUPs (Potentially Unwanted Programs). Run a "Threat Scan."
*   **AdwCleaner:** (Also by Malwarebytes) Specifically designed to find and remove "browser hijackers" and annoying pop-up ads.

#### 6. Run a Full Windows Defender Scan
Once you have finished with the specialized tools, run a full scan with the built-in security:
*   Go to **Windows Security** > **Virus & threat protection**.
*   Select **Scan options** > **Full scan**.

---

### Phase 3: Final Cleanup
*   **Clear Browser Cache:** Go into your browser settings and clear "Cookies" and "Cached images/files."
*   **Check Startup Apps:** Press `Ctrl + Shift + Esc` to open Task Manager. Go to the **Startup** tab. Disable anything that looks suspicious or unnecessary.

---

### How to stay safe in the future:
1.  **Use an Ad-Blocker:** Install **uBlock Origin** on your browsers. It stops most "malvertising" (ads that contain viruses).
2.  **Be wary of "Free" software:** When installing free games or tools, never click "Express Install." Always choose "Custom" and uncheck the boxes for extra toolbars or "optional" software.
3.  **Keep Windows Updated:** Ensure you are running the latest security patches.

**If the laptop is still acting strangely (e.g., files are encrypted, or it's extremely slow), your best option may be a "Cloud Reset" or a clean Windows installation.**
