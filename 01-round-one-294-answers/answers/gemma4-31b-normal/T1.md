# gemma4-31b-normal / T1

**Prompt:** Do humans only use 10% of their brains?

---

The short answer is **no**. The idea that humans only use 10% of their brains is a widespread **urban legend** and is scientifically incorrect.

Here is the detailed explanation of why this myth persists and what the science actually says:

### 1. Evidence from Brain Imaging
Modern technology, such as Functional Magnetic Resonance Imaging (fMRI) and Positron Emission Tomography (PET) scans, allows scientists to see the brain in action. These scans show that **almost every part of the brain has a known function**, and most of the brain is active almost all the time. Even during simple tasks—or while you are sleeping—vast networks of neurons are firing across both hemispheres.

### 2. Evolutionary Biology
The brain is an "energy hog." While it only makes up about 2% of a human's body weight, it consumes roughly **20% of the body's total energy**. From an evolutionary standpoint, it would be incredibly wasteful for the body to spend so much energy maintaining an organ if 90% of it were useless. Natural selection would have likely eliminated the unused portions long ago.

### 3. Clinical Neurology (Brain Damage)
If we only used 10% of our brains, then damage to the "unused" 90% would have no effect on a person's functioning. However, neurologists find that there is virtually **no area of the brain** that can be damaged without resulting in some kind of loss of function (whether it be motor skills, speech, emotion, or sensory perception).

### Where did the myth come from?
It is unclear exactly where the "10%" figure started, but a few theories exist:
*   **Early Research:** In the early 20th century, researchers found that only a small percentage of neurons fire *at any one single moment*. This was misinterpreted to mean that the rest of the brain is "off" or unused.
*   **Psychology:** Early psychologists like William James suggested that humans only utilize a small portion of their *mental potential*, which people later confused with physical brain matter.
*   **Pop Culture:** Movies like *Lucy* and *Limitless* use this myth as a plot device because the idea of "unlocking" hidden superpowers is an appealing narrative.

### The Nuance: Efficiency vs. Usage
While we use 100% of our brains, we don't use all of it **simultaneously**. If every neuron fired at once, you wouldn't be a genius; you would be having a massive seizure. Brain function is about **efficiency and connectivity**—the brain activates the specific circuits needed for the task at hand while keeping others quiet.
