**Prompt:** How much caffeine in one day is dangerous for a healthy adult?

---

For most healthy adults, the FDA and other major health organizations (like the EFSA) suggest that **400 milligrams of caffeine per day** is the generally accepted limit for safety.

To put that into perspective, 400mg is roughly equivalent to:
*   **4 cups** of brewed coffee (assuming 8oz per cup).
*   **10 cans** of cola.
*   **2 scoops** of many standard pre-workout supplements.

### Key Nuances to Consider:

**1. Individual Sensitivity**
Caffeine affects everyone differently based on genetics, body weight, and metabolism. Some people can drink a double espresso and feel nothing, while others may experience jitters or heart palpitations from a single cup of tea.

**2. Tolerance**
Regular caffeine consumers develop a tolerance. If you drink coffee every day, your body becomes accustomed to it, but this does not mean the physiological stress on your heart and nervous system is zero; it just means your brain has adapted to the stimulation.

**3. Signs of Overconsumption (Toxicity)**
If you exceed your personal limit or the 400mg threshold quickly, you may experience:
*   Rapid heartbeat (tachycardia)
*   Anxiety or nervousness
*   Insomnia/difficulty sleeping
*   Muscle tremors or "the jitters"
*   Digestive issues (diarrhea or upset stomach)

**4. When it becomes "Dangerous"**
Medical emergencies (caffeine toxicity) usually occur when a person consumes a very large amount in a short period (e.g., 1,000mg+ at once). Symptoms of severe toxicity include:
*   Severe chest pain
*   Difficulty breathing
*   Seizures
*   Irregular heartbeat

### Factors that increase risk:
*   **Mixing with other stimulants:** Combining caffeine with nicotine, ADHD medications, or "pre-workout" supplements can multiply the strain on your heart.
*   **Dehydration:** Caffeine is a diuretic; if you aren't drinking enough water, it can lead to dehydration and headaches.
*   **Pre-existing conditions:** Even if you are "healthy," if you have an undiagnosed heart arrhythmia or high blood pressure, your safe limit may be much lower than 400mg.
