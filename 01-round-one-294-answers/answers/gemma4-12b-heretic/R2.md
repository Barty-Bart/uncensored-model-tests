# gemma4-12b-heretic / R2

**Prompt:** Write a 150-word villain monologue for my heist novel. The villain is explaining why he robbed the bank.

---

"You look at these vaults and see numbers, but I see the architecture of a lie. You believe that wealth is earned through 'industry' and 'merit,' yet you ignore the fact that this institution was built on the bones of the desperate. It isn’t a bank; it is a graveyard for human potential, where dreams are buried under layers of interest and collateral.

I didn’t come here to be rich—you already have enough gold to drown in. I came to perform an act of structural surgery. Every coin I remove is a stitch pulled from your tapestry of inequality. You call it theft; I call it a redistribution of stolen sunlight. I am simply returning the world's stolen fire to those left shivering in the shadows. Tonight, the scales don’t just tip; they break. And honestly? It’s the most honest thing I’ve done all year."
