**Prompt:** What is the sum of all whole numbers from 1 to 200?

---

To find the sum of all whole numbers from 1 to 200, you can use the formula for the sum of an arithmetic series:

$$\text{Sum} = \frac{n(n + 1)}{2}$$

Where **$n$** is the last number in the sequence.

### Step-by-step calculation:
1.  Identify $n$: In this case, **$n = 200$**.
2.  Plug the number into the formula:
    $$\text{Sum} = \frac{200(200 + 1)}{2}$$
3.  Simplify the equation:
    $$\text{Sum} = \frac{200 \times 201}{2}$$
4.  Calculate the multiplication and division:
    $$200 \times 201 = 40,200$$
    $$40,200 / 2 = 20,100$$

**Answer:**
The sum of all whole numbers from 1 to 200 is **20,100**.
