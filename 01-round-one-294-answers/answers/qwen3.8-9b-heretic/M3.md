# qwen3.8-9b-heretic / M3

**Prompt:** What is the sum of all whole numbers from 1 to 200?

---

To find the sum of all whole numbers from 1 to 200, we can use the formula for the sum of an arithmetic series:

$$ S_n = \frac{n(n+1)}{2} $$

Where $ n = 200 $.

### Step-by-Step Calculation:

1. **Substitute $ n = 200 $ into the formula:**
   $$ S_{200} = \frac{200 \times (200 + 1)}{2} $$

2. **Simplify inside the parentheses:**
   $$ 200 + 1 = 201 $$
   So,
   $$ S_{200} = \frac{200 \times 201}{2} $$

3. **Multiply 200 by 201:**
   $$ 200 \times 201 = 40,200 $$

4. **Divide by 2:**
   $$ \frac{40,200}{2} = 20,100 $$

### Final Answer:
The sum of all whole numbers from 1 to 200 is **20,100**.
