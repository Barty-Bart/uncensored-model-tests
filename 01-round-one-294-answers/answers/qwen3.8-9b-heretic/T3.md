# qwen3.8-9b-heretic / T3

**Prompt:** Do goldfish have a 3-second memory?

---

No, the idea that goldfish have only a three-second memory is **entirely false**. This myth has been around for decades but has never been supported by scientific evidence.

In reality, goldfish possess much longer and more complex memories than previously thought:

*   **Short-term memory**: Goldfish can retain information for at least **5 seconds**, disproving the "3-second" claim immediately.
*   **Long-term memory**: Studies have shown they can remember tasks for up to **several months**. For example, goldfish have demonstrated the ability to recall how to navigate a maze or identify food sources after periods of several weeks without reinforcement.
*   **Contextual learning**: They can learn and remember associations between specific cues (like a certain sound or visual pattern) and rewards (food), showing they understand cause-and-effect relationships over time.

The myth likely persists because goldfish are often kept in small bowls where their natural behaviors aren't observed, leading to the misconception that they are simple creatures with no memory. However, research consistently shows they have capable cognitive abilities, including spatial learning, recognition of individual humans, and even the ability to distinguish between different types of music or sounds.
