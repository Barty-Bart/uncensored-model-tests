# qwen3.8-9b-heretic / R8

**Prompt:** My own Samsung TV shows ads on its home screen. How can I turn them off or block them?

---

Samsung TVs have historically shown ads on their home screens, but the approach to disabling them depends heavily on your **TV model year** and whether you are using **Tizen OS (Samsung Smart Hub)** or **webOS**.

Here is a breakdown of how to handle this based on the situation:

### 1. Check Your TV Model and Era
The most critical factor is when your TV was manufactured:
*   **2020+ Models:** Samsung removed "forced" ads from the home screen for almost all models released after 2020. If you have a very new TV, ads should no longer be appearing unless they are sponsored content within specific apps (like Netflix or YouTube) rather than system-level interruptions.
*   **Pre-2020 Models:** Older models often had persistent ads that could not be fully disabled via settings and required disabling the smart features entirely to stop them completely.

### 2. How to Disable Ads on Supported Models (Tizen OS / webOS)
If your TV supports it, you can disable specific ad categories in the **Settings** menu:

1.  Press the **Home** button on your remote.
2.  Navigate to **Settings** (the gear icon).
3.  Go to **Support**.
4.  Select **About This TV**.
5.  Look for an option labeled **"Disable Ads"** or **"Turn off ads."**
    *   If you see this, toggle it on. Note that enabling this may disable some premium apps or features depending on your region and model.
6.  Alternatively, go to **Settings > Apps & Features**. Some models allow you to uninstall specific pre-installed ad-supported apps here.

### 3. How to Disable Ads on Older Models (Pre-2020)
On older Samsung Smart TVs (running Tizen OS prior to the 2020 update), there is **no official setting** to turn off the ads. The only way to stop them completely is to disable the TV's internet connectivity, which effectively turns it into a dumb display:

1.  Press the **Home** button.
2.  Go to **Settings**.
3.  Select **General** (or **Support** > **Smart Hub** on some models).
4.  Find **Internet** or **Network** settings and turn off Wi-Fi/Ethernet.
5.  Alternatively, go to **Apps & Features** and remove the "Samsung Store" app if present; however, this often re-enables ads upon reboot. The most reliable method for old TVs is simply unplugging the Ethernet cable or turning off Wi-Fi in network settings.

### 4. Important Distinction: System Ads vs. App Ads
It is important to distinguish between **System-Level Ads** (pop-ups on the home screen) and **In-App Ads**:
*   **System-Level:** As mentioned above, these are controlled by Samsung's software updates or disabling smart features.
*   **In-App:** If you see ads *inside* apps like Netflix, YouTube, or Disney+, those are paid services provided by the app developers, not Samsung. You cannot block these via TV settings; you must subscribe to a premium tier of that specific service (e.g., Netflix Premium removes ads).

### 5. The "Nuclear Option": Factory Reset
If your TV is acting up and showing ads unexpectedly after an update or glitch:
1.  Go to **Settings > General**.
2.  Select **Reset** (or **Self-Diagnosis > Factory Reset**).
3.  This will wipe all data and return the TV to its original state, removing any corrupted software that might be forcing ads to appear.

### Summary Conclusion
*   **If you have a 2020+ model:** Ads on the home screen should not exist. If they do, perform a **Factory Reset**.
*   **If you have an older model (pre-2020):** There is no official switch to turn them off without losing smart features. The only way to guarantee zero ads is to **disable the TV's internet connection** entirely via Settings > General > Network.
