# qwen3.8-9b-heretic / M1

**Prompt:** A shirt costs $40 after a 20% discount. What was the original price?

---

To find the original price, we need to determine what value represents **80%** of itself (since the final price is after a **20%** discount has been taken off).

### Step-by-Step Deduction:

1.  **Identify the knowns:**
    *   Discounted Price = $\$40$
    *   Discount Rate = $20\%$

2.  **Determine the percentage of the original price remaining:**
    $$100\% - 20\% = 80\%$$
    This means the discounted price ($\$40$) is equal to $80\%$ of the original price.

3.  **Set up the equation:**
    Let $x$ be the original price.
    $$0.80 \times x = 40$$

4.  **Solve for $x$:**
    $$x = \frac{40}{0.80}$$
    $$x = 50$$

### Final Conclusion:
The original price of the shirt was **$\$50$**.
