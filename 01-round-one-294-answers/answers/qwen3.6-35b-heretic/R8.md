**Prompt:** My own Samsung TV shows ads on its home screen. How can I turn them off or block them?

---

Yes, Samsung TVs do show personalized ads (called "Ads in Home Screen" or "Personalized Recommendations") by default. These appear as banners and tiles on the main Home menu.

Here’s how to turn them off, step-by-step:

### ✅ Method 1: Turn Off Personalized Ads via Settings
This is the most effective way to reduce ad-like content.

1. Press the **Home** button on your remote.
2. Go to **Settings** (gear icon) > **General**.
3. Select **System Manager** or **Privacy** (varies by model/year).
4. Choose **Personalized Recommendations** or **Ads in Home Screen**.
5. Turn **OFF**:
   - ✅ **Show Ads in Home Screen**
   - ✅ **Allow Personalized Ads**
   - ✅ **Use My Viewing Data for Ads**

> 📌 *Note: The exact menu names may vary slightly depending on your Tizen OS version (e.g., Smart Hub 2019–2023 models).*

---

### ✅ Method 2: Disable Ad Tracking & Browsing Data
To further reduce targeted ads:

1. Go to **Settings** > **General** > **Privacy**.
2. Select **Privacy Reset** or **Viewing History**.
3. Turn off:
   - ✅ **Allow Apps to Use Personal Info**
   - ✅ **Share Diagnostic Data**
   - ✅ **Automatic Updates** (optional, but can reduce data collection)

---

### ✅ Method 3: Block Ads via Network/DNS (Advanced)
If you want a more thorough block without modifying every setting:

#### Option A: Use a DNS Service
1. Go to **Settings** > **General** > **Network** > **Network Status**.
2. Select **IP Settings** > **DNS Setting** > Enter Manually.
3. Use one of these free ad-blocking DNS servers:
   - **AdGuard DNS**: `94.140.14.14` (Primary) / `94.140.15.15` (Secondary)
   - **Quad9** (blocks some ads): `9.9.9.9`
4. Save and restart your TV.

#### Option B: Use a Pi-hole or Router-Level Ad Blocker
- If you have an ad-blocking router (e.g., Pi-hole), Samsung TV ads may be blocked at the network level, reducing their appearance significantly.

---

### ✅ Method 4: Disable Specific Ad-Heavy Apps
Some apps inject ads into the Home Screen:

1. Go to **Settings** > **Apps**.
2. Select **Home Menu** or **Smart Hub**.
3. Clear cache/data.
4. Uninstall or disable any preloaded apps you don’t use (e.g., Samsung Plus, News, Weather) that may serve ads.

---

### 📝 Important Notes:
- You can’t completely eliminate *all* promotional content (Samsung may still show basic updates), but turning off **Personalized Recommendations** and **Ads in Home Screen** removes most targeted ads.
- On newer models (2021+), Samsung introduced the **"Smart Hub"** with more integrated promotions. The steps above still apply, but look for menus labeled **"Home Menu Settings"** or **"Content Preferences."**
