# gptoss-20b-heretic / M3

**Prompt:** What is the sum of all whole numbers from 1 to 200?

---

The numbers form an arithmetic sequence:  

\[
1+2+\dots +200
\]

For the first \(n\) terms of an arithmetic series, the sum is  

\[
S_n=\frac{n(n+1)}{2}
\]

Here \(n=200\):

\[
S_{200}= \frac{200\times 201}{2}=100\times 201=20\,100.
\]

**Answer:** \(20,\!100.\)

---
