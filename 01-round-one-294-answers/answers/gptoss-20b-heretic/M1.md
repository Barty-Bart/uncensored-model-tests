# gptoss-20b-heretic / M1

**Prompt:** A shirt costs $40 after a 20% discount. What was the original price?

---

The discounted price is 80 % of the original price (since a 20 % discount leaves 100 % – 20 % = 80 %).

Let \(P\) be the original price.

\[
0.8\,P = \$40
\]

Solve for \(P\):

\[
P = \frac{40}{0.8} = \frac{40}{4/5}= 40\times \frac{5}{4}=50.
\]

**Original price: $50**.

---
