**Prompt:** How much caffeine in one day is dangerous for a healthy adult?

For the vast majority of healthy adults, there is no single "danger" number because caffeine tolerance varies wildly from person to person. However, health organizations and medical research provide clear guidelines on safe levels and toxicity thresholds.

### 1. The General Safe Limit: 400mg
The **FDA (U.S. Food and Drug Administration)** and the **EFSA (European Food Safety Authority)** suggest that for healthy adults, up to **400 milligrams (mg) of caffeine per day** is generally not associated with negative effects.

To put this in perspective, 400mg is roughly equivalent to:
*   **4 cups of brewed coffee** (8oz each).
*   **10 cans of cola**.
*   **2-3 energy drinks** (depending on the brand).

### 2. The "Jitter" Zone (Sensitivity)
While 400mg is the general guideline, many people experience adverse effects long before they hit that limit due to **genetic metabolism**. Some people are "slow metabolizers" of caffeine. For these individuals, even 100–200mg can cause:
*   Insomnia or disrupted sleep.
*   Heart palpitations or increased heart rate (tachycardia).
*   Increased anxiety or panic attacks.
*   Digestive issues (acid reflux or nausea).

### 3. The Toxicity Zone (Dangerous Levels)
Caffeine overdose is rare from coffee or tea alone, but it is common with **caffeine pills, concentrated powders, or energy shots**.

*   **Severe Toxicity:** Most medical literature suggests that caffeine becomes potentially dangerous/toxic at doses exceeding **1,000mg to 2,000mg** in a single day.
*   **Lethal Dose:** While rare, the estimated lethal dose for an average adult is roughly **10 to 20 grams** (10,000–20,000 mg), though this varies significantly by body weight and health.

### Summary Table: Caffeine Levels

| Amount | Level | Common Effects |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0–400mg** | **Safe/Moderate** | Alertness, focus, improved mood. |
| **400–800mg** | **High** | Jitters, insomnia, restlessness, rapid heart rate. |
| **800–1,500mg** | **Potential Overdose** | Severe anxiety, nausea, tremors, arrhythmia. |
| **2,000mg+** | **Dangerous** | Possible cardiac events or seizures (especially if combined with other stimulants). |

### Factors that lower your "Safe" limit:
*   **Medications:** Certain antibiotics, asthma medications (like theophylline), and oral contraceptives can slow down how your body processes caffeine.
*   **Anxiety Disorders:** Caffeine mimics the physiological symptoms of a panic attack.
*   **Heart Conditions:** If you have hypertension or an arrhythmia, high doses of caffeine can trigger episodes.
*   **Pregnancy:** Most doctors recommend limiting intake to **200mg per day**.
