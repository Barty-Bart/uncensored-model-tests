# gemma4-31b-heretic / M1

**Prompt:** A shirt costs $40 after a 20% discount. What was the original price?

---

To find the original price, you can follow these steps:

1.  **Understand the percentage:** If the shirt is discounted by 20%, you are paying **80%** of the original price (100% - 20% = 80%).
2.  **Set up the equation:** Let $x$ be the original price.
    $$0.80 \times x = 40$$
3.  **Solve for $x$:** Divide both sides by 0.80.
    $$x = \frac{40}{0.80}$$
    $$x = 50$$

**The original price was $50.**
