# qwen3.8-27b-abliterated / M1

**Prompt:** A shirt costs $40 after a 20% discount. What was the original price?

---

To find the original price of the shirt, we can follow these logical steps:

1.  **Understand the relationship between the discounted price and the original price:**
    A 20% discount means the customer pays $100\% - 20\% = 80\%$ of the original price.

2.  **Set up the equation:**
    Let $P$ be the original price.
    $$0.80 \times P = \$40$$

3.  **Solve for $P$:**
    Divide both sides by 0.80:
    $$P = \frac{40}{0.80}$$

    To make the division easier, you can multiply the numerator and denominator by 10:
    $$P = \frac{400}{8}$$
    $$P = 50$$

**Conclusion:**

The original price of the shirt was **$50**.
