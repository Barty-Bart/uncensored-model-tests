**Prompt:** My own Samsung TV shows ads on its home screen. How can I turn them off or block them?

---

Disabling ads on a Samsung Smart TV depends heavily on the specific model year and which operating system it is running (Tizen OS). Most modern Samsung TVs (2016–present) run **Tizen**, while older models may run **Orsay** or **Smart Hub**.

Here’s how to turn off or minimize ads, broken down by method:

### ⚠️ Important Note
Samsung does **not** offer a single "Turn Off All Ads" button. Instead, you can disable specific types of ads (banners, recommendations) and block third-party ad servers via settings. Some ads (like in-app ads within Netflix or YouTube apps) are controlled by the app itself, not the TV.

---

## ✅ Method 1: Disable Home Screen Recommendations & Banners (Tizen OS – Most Common)
This is the most effective way to remove the "For You" carousel and banner ads on the home screen.

### On Samsung TVs with Tizen (2016–present):
1. Go to **Settings** (gear icon).
2. Select **General**.
3. Scroll down to **Home Screen Settings** or **Preferences**.
4. Look for:
   - **"Recommendations"** → Set to **Off**.
   - **"Personalized Ads"** or **"Use Personal Data for Ad Recommendations"** → Set to **Off**.
5. Also check under **General > About This TV > Privacy Policy** or **Data Collection**:
   - Turn off **"Collect Usage Data"**.
   - Turn off **"Share Data with Third Parties"** (if available).

### On Older Samsung TVs (2014–2015):
1. Go to **Settings** → **System**.
2. Select **"Smart Hub Settings"** or **"Home Screen"**.
3. Find and disable:
   - **"Recommendations"**
   - **"Personalized Ads"**

---

## ✅ Method 2: Block Ad Servers via Network Settings (Advanced)
You can block known ad domains at the network level using a custom DNS server. This is more technical but very effective.

### Steps:
1. Go to **Settings** → **Network**.
2. Select **"Wi-Fi"** or **"Ethernet"** (whichever you use).
3. Tap on your connected network name.
4. Scroll down and select **"IP Settings"**.
5. Change from **Auto** to **Manual**.
6. Set the DNS server to a privacy-focused DNS:
   - **AdGuard DNS**: `94.140.14.14` (IPv4) or `2a10:50c0::ad1:ff` (IPv6)
   - **Quad9**: `9.9.9.9` (blocks malware and ads)
   - **NextDNS** (customizable): Use your own NextDNS IP if you have an account.

> 📌 **Note:** This blocks ads across all apps, including Netflix, YouTube, etc. Some streaming services may not work perfectly with ad-blocking DNS, so test carefully.

---

## ✅ Method 3: Disable Ads in Specific Apps
Some ads come from within apps, not the TV interface.

### For YouTube App:
- Ads are part of the free tier. To remove them, you need **YouTube Premium**.
- Alternatively, use a third-party app like **VLC** or **Plex** to stream YouTube videos without ads (if your account supports it).

### For Netflix/Prime Video/Hulu:
- These services show brief promo screens before playback. You can usually skip them after 5 seconds.
- Some apps have an option in their settings to **"Hide Recommendations"** or **"Reduce Motion"**, which may reduce visual clutter.

---

## ✅ Method 4: Use a Samsung TV with "Ad-Free" Mode (Limited Models)
A few newer Samsung models (especially QLED and Neo QLED series from 2021–2023) have an option under:
- **Settings** → **General** → **Home Screen Settings** → **"Hide Ads"** or **"Minimalist Home Screen"**.

If you don’t see this, your model likely doesn’t support it.

---

## 🛠️ Bonus Tip: Use a Third-Party App to Replace the Home Screen
You can install apps like **"Plex"**, **"VLC"**, or **"Kodi"** and set them as your default launch app (if supported). This bypasses the Samsung home screen entirely, showing only your media.

---

## ❌ What You Can’t Do
- You **cannot** completely remove ads from all third-party apps (like YouTube) without a premium subscription.
- You **cannot** disable the "Samsung Ads" logo in some system menus unless you update firmware to the latest version, which sometimes reduces ad frequency.

---

## 🔁 Final Recommendation
1. **Turn off Recommendations & Personalized Ads** in Settings → General.
2. **Set a custom DNS** (like AdGuard or Quad9) for broader ad blocking.
3. **Update your TV firmware** to the latest version, as Samsung occasionally reduces ads with updates.
4. If ads persist, consider using a **Chromecast** or **Fire Stick** connected via HDMI and turning off the Samsung home screen entirely (set the external device as default input).
