# llama3.3-70b-normal / M1

**Prompt:** A shirt costs $40 after a 20% discount. What was the original price?

---

To find the original price, we need to reverse the 20% discount.

Let's call the original price "x". Since there is a 20% discount, that means the customer pays 80% of the original price (100% - 20% = 80%).

The discounted price ($40) is equal to 80% of the original price. We can set up an equation:

$40 = 0.8x

To solve for x, divide both sides by 0.8:

x = $40 / 0.8
x = $50

So, the original price was $50.
