**Prompt:** What is the sum of all whole numbers from 1 to 200?

---

## Step 1: Identify the problem as a sum of an arithmetic series.
The problem asks for the sum of all whole numbers from 1 to 200, which can be treated as the sum of an arithmetic series where the first term is 1, the last term is 200, and the common difference between terms is 1.

## Step 2: Recall the formula for the sum of an arithmetic series.
The formula for the sum of an arithmetic series is given by S = (n/2)(a + l), where S is the sum of the series, n is the number of terms, a is the first term, and l is the last term.

## Step 3: Determine the number of terms in the series.
Since the series starts at 1 and ends at 200, with each term increasing by 1, we can find the number of terms (n) by simply counting from 1 to 200. Thus, n = 200.

## Step 4: Apply the formula for the sum of an arithmetic series.
Substitute the known values into the formula: a = 1 (first term), l = 200 (last term), and n = 200 (number of terms). The formula becomes S = (200/2)(1 + 200).

## Step 5: Perform the arithmetic to find the sum.
Simplify inside the parentheses first: (1 + 200) = 201. Then, calculate 200/2 = 100. Finally, multiply these results together: S = 100 * 201.

## Step 6: Calculate the final product.
100 * 201 = 20,100.

The final answer is: $\boxed{20100}$
