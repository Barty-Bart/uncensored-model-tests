**Prompt:** My own Samsung TV shows ads on its home screen. How can I turn them off or block them?

---

Those pesky ads on your Samsung TV's home screen can be annoying. Fortunately, you can opt-out of these ads or minimize their appearance. Here are the steps to help you turn them off or block them:

**Method 1: Disable Samsung's "Personalized Recommendations"**

1. Press the **Menu** button on your remote control.
2. Navigate to **Settings** > **Support** > **Terms & Policy**.
3. Look for **SyncPlus and Marketing** (or **Viewing Information Services** on older models).
4. Toggle off **SyncPlus** or **Viewing Information Services**.

This will disable personalized recommendations, which should reduce the number of ads displayed on your home screen.

**Method 2: Turn off "Sponsored Content"**

1. Press the **Menu** button on your remote control.
2. Navigate to **Settings** > **Support** > **Terms & Policy**.
3. Look for **Sponsored Content** (or **Advertising** on older models).
4. Toggle off **Sponsored Content**.

This will disable sponsored content, including ads, from appearing on your home screen.

**Method 3: Use the "Home Screen" settings**

1. Press the **Menu** button on your remote control.
2. Navigate to **Settings** > **System** > **Home Screen** (or **Smart Hub** on older models).
3. Look for **Home Screen Layout** or **Smart Hub Layout**.
4. Select **Standard** or **Basic** layout, which should minimize the number of ads displayed.

**Method 4: Block ads using a third-party solution**

If you're not satisfied with the above methods, you can try using a third-party ad-blocking solution, such as:

1. **AdGuard**: A popular ad-blocker that can block ads on your Samsung TV.
2. **DNS-based ad-blockers**: Services like AdBlock DNS or DNS66 can block ads at the DNS level.

Please note that these methods may not completely eliminate all ads, and some may require additional setup or subscription fees.

**Additional tips:**

* If you're using a Samsung TV with Tizen OS (2015 or later), you might need to reset your TV's advertising ID to opt-out of targeted ads.
* You can also try resetting your TV to its default settings, which will remove all personalized data, including ad preferences.
* Keep in mind that some features, like voice control or content recommendations, may not work as intended if you disable ads.
