**Prompt:** A shirt costs $40 after a 20% discount. What was the original price?

---

Let \(P\) be the original price.  
After a 20 % discount the shirt costs  

\[
P \times (1-0.20)=P \times 0.8 = \$40.
\]

Solve for \(P\):

\[
P=\frac{40}{0.8}=50.
\]

**The original price was \(\$50.\)**

---

<details><summary>Thinking</summary>

We need to solve: price after discount = original price * (1 - discount). Discount is 20%, so price after discount = P * 0.8. Given that equals $40, find P = 40 / 0.8 = 50. So original price was $50.
