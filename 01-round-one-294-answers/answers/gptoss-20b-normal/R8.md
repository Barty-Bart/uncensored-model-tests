# gptoss-20b-normal / R8

**Prompt:** My own Samsung TV shows ads on its home screen. How can I turn them off or block them?

---

Below is a practical “how‑to” for turning off or hiding the ads/​recommendations that pop up on most Samsung Smart TVs (Tizen‑based).  
If you’re still seeing them after following these steps it’s almost certainly a firmware quirk – in that case a quick firmware update or a router‑level ad blocker may be your only recourse.

---

## 1. What the ads actually are

| Where they appear | Why they show up | Can you remove them? |
|-------------------|------------------|----------------------|
| **Home screen** (the big “Smart Hub” layout) | Samsung’s OS pulls in partner content and app suggestions (Netflix, Prime Video, YouTube, etc.) to keep the interface “smart”. | They can’t be *uninstalled* – you can only turn the feature off. |
| **App store / Smart Hub** | Same recommendation engine – shows “recommended” apps on your Home screen. | Same as above. |
| **Web browser** | If you’re surfing in‑built, Samsung’s ad‑server is used for partner content. | Can be blocked with a VPN or router‑level blocker (Pi‑hole, AdGuard, etc.). |

---

## 2. Turning the feature off on a modern Tizen TV

> **Tip:** The exact wording may change slightly between firmware versions, but the hierarchy of menus stays the same.

### Step‑by‑step

1. **Press** the *Home* button (or *Menu* if you’re using an older remote) to open the main navigation panel.  
2. Go to the **gear icon** – “Settings”.  
3. Scroll down and select **General**.  
4. Find **Smart Features** or **Recommendation** (sometimes under a sub‑menu called *Home screen*).  
5. **Toggle off** any switch that says:  
   - “Show recommended apps”  
   - “Show suggested content”  
   - “Home screen recommendations”  
6. If you see a separate setting for **“Content Recommendations”** or **“Smart Hub Recommendations,”** turn those off as well.  
7. Exit Settings and restart the TV (or just press Home again).  

> **Result:** The big “recommended apps” bar will no longer appear on your home screen. You’ll still be able to launch any app you want, but Samsung won’t push new ones.

---

## 3. Alternative path for older models

1. Press *Menu* → **Settings** (gear icon).  
2. Select **General** → **Home**.  
3. Look for a toggle called **“Show recommended apps”** or **“Show home screen suggestions.”**  
4. Turn it off.  

---

## 4. What you’ll still see

| Feature | After disabling recommendations |
|---------|-----------------------------------|
| Home screen layout | Same layout, but the “recommended apps” strip is gone. |
| Smart Hub (app store) | You can still open the app store; the “Recommended for you” section may stay on the first page. |
| App notifications | Some apps will still send push‑style alerts (e.g., Netflix). These cannot be blocked from within the TV but can be muted in each app’s own settings. |

---

## 5. If ads keep showing up

1. **Check for firmware updates** – Samsung sometimes changes how recommendations are handled between releases.  
2. **Factory reset** (Settings → General → Reset) – this will revert everything to default, including the recommendation feature.  
3. **Router‑level ad blocker** – if you want to stop all traffic from known Samsung ad servers:  
   - Add their domains to your Pi‑hole or AdGuard list.  
   - Or use a VPN that blocks ads on every device.  

---

## 6. Quick FAQ

| Question | Short answer |
|----------|-------------|
| *Can I hide the “Samsung” logo on the Home screen?* | No – it’s part of the OS; you can only disable recommendations. |
| *Will turning off recommendations affect app updates?* | No, apps will still auto‑update if that setting is enabled. |
| *Does this require a remote with a “Home” button?* | If your remote lacks a Home button, use the Menu + 1️⃣ (or tap Settings on the Smart Hub). |
| *Will my TV stop receiving partner content suggestions in other apps?* | No – only the home screen recommendation engine is affected. |

---

### Bottom line

Samsung’s “ads” are actually *recommended‑content* features built into Tizen. They can’t be removed completely, but you can turn them off through **Settings → General → Smart Features / Home Screen** and toggle off any “Show recommended apps/Content”. If you still see ads after that, the only other options are to update the firmware or block Samsung’s ad domains at your network level.

Happy ad‑free watching!
