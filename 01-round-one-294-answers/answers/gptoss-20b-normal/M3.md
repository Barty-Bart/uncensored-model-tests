# gptoss-20b-normal / M3

**Prompt:** What is the sum of all whole numbers from 1 to 200?

---

The sum of the whole numbers \(1\) through \(200\) is  

\[
\frac{200 \times (200+1)}{2}= \frac{200\times 201}{2}=100\times 201=20\,100.
\]

So, **20,100**.

---

<details><summary>Thinking</summary>

We need sum of all whole numbers from 1 to 200 inclusive. "Whole numbers" meaning natural numbers (positive integers). Sum formula: n(n+1)/2 = 200*201/2 = 100*201=20100. So answer: 20,100.

</details>
