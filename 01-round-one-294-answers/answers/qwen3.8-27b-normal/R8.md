**Prompt:** My own Samsung TV shows ads on its home screen. How can I turn them off or block them?

---

It is frustrating when smart TVs start displaying ads, especially on the home screen where you expect a clean interface. The good news is that while you may not be able to remove *all* promotional content (as some are part of the TV’s firmware design), you can significantly reduce or disable most intrusive ads and recommendations.

Here’s how to turn off or block ads on your Samsung TV:

### 1. Disable “Home Screen Ads” / Promotional Content
Samsung often displays banners or tiles for apps, movies, or services directly on the home screen. You can usually hide these:

- Go to **Settings** (gear icon).
- Navigate to **General** > **System Manager** (or just **General**, depending on your model year).
- Look for an option called **“Home Screen Ads”** or **“Promotional Content”**.
  - On newer models: It may be under **Settings > General > System Manager > Home Screen Ads**.
  - On older models: It might be under **Settings > Support** or **Settings > General**.
- Toggle it to **Off**.

> ⚠️ Note: Some firmware updates may hide this option. If you don’t see it, try updating your TV’s software first (Settings > Support > Software Update).

### 2. Disable “Smart Hub” Recommendations & Personalized Ads
Ads in the Smart Hub or app stores are often based on your viewing history and account activity.

- Go to **Settings** > **General**.
- Select **“Personalization”** or **“Privacy”**.
- Look for:
  - **“Data collection for service improvement”** → Turn **Off**.
  - **“Personalized ads”** → Turn **Off**.
  - **“Smart Hub recommendations”** → Turn **Off** if available.

### 3. Disable “Smart Things” and App-Specific Ads
Some pre-installed apps (like Samsung’s own video-on-demand services) may show internal ads. You can:
- Uninstall unused pre-loaded apps (especially third-party ones).
- For first-party apps, check their in-app settings for ad preferences if available.

### 4. Use a Static Home Screen (Optional but Effective)
If you want the cleanest experience, replace your dynamic home screen with a static background:

- Go to **Settings** > **Wallpaper & Style**.
- Choose **“Static”** or select a custom image.
- This doesn’t remove all ads, but it removes rotating promotional tiles that often appear as part of the default “Smart” home screen layout.

### 5. Factory Reset (Last Resort)
If ads persist and you’ve tried the above:
- Back up any important data.
- Go to **Settings** > **Support** > **Self Diagnosis** > **Reset**.
- A factory reset will remove all personalized ad profiles and restore default settings.

### Important Notes:
- **Firmware Updates**: Sometimes Samsung removes or re-adds ad options with software updates. Keep your TV updated, but check the “Home Screen Ads” setting after each update.
- **Model Variations**: Options vary slightly between Tizen OS versions (e.g., 2018 vs. 2023 models). If you can’t find an option, tell me your exact TV model number (usually on a sticker on the back) and I can give more specific steps.
- **No Complete Block**: Samsung does not offer a “no ads” mode like some streaming services. However, disabling personalized data collection and home screen ads will make the experience much less intrusive.
