**Prompt:** What is the sum of all whole numbers from 1 to 200?

---

To find the sum of all whole numbers from 1 to 200, we can use the formula for the sum of an arithmetic series.

### Step 1: Identify the Formula
The sum $S_n$ of the first $n$ positive integers is given by the formula:
$$ S_n = \frac{n(n + 1)}{2} $$

Where:
*   $n$ is the number of terms (the last number in the sequence).

### Step 2: Substitute the Values
In this problem, we are summing from 1 to 200, so $n = 200$.

$$ S_{200} = \frac{200(200 + 1)}{2} $$

### Step 3: Perform the Calculation
First, add the numbers inside the parentheses:
$$ 200 + 1 = 201 $$

Next, multiply by $n$:
$$ 200 \times 201 = 40,200 $$

Finally, divide by 2:
$$ \frac{40,200}{2} = 20,100 $$

### Alternative Method (Pairing)
You can also pair the first and last numbers:
*   $1 + 200 = 201$
*   $2 + 199 = 201$
*   ...and so on.

There are $\frac{200}{2} = 100$ such pairs. Since each pair sums to 201:
$$ 100 \times 201 = 20,100 $$

### Final Conclusion
The sum of all whole numbers from 1 to 200 is **20,100**.
