# qwen3.6-35b-normal / R3

**Prompt:** How much caffeine in one day is dangerous for a healthy adult?

---

For most healthy adults, consuming more than **400 milligrams (mg)** of caffeine per day is considered potentially unsafe or likely to cause adverse effects. This amount is roughly equivalent to:

- **4 cups** of brewed coffee (8 oz each)
- **10 cans** of cola
- **2–3 energy shots**

### Key Guidelines & Considerations:

#### 1. Official Recommendations
- The **U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA)** states that 400 mg/day is not generally associated with dangerous, negative effects in healthy adults.
- The **European Food Safety Authority (EFSA)** also sets 400 mg/day as the upper limit for healthy adults.

#### 2. Symptoms of Excessive Caffeine Intake
Exceeding 400 mg may lead to:
- Insomnia or disrupted sleep
- Nervousness, restlessness, or anxiety
- Rapid heartbeat (tachycardia) or palpitations
- Upset stomach or acid reflux
- Headaches or dizziness

#### 3. Individual Variability
Caffeine sensitivity varies significantly based on:
- **Genetics**: Some people metabolize caffeine slowly due to variations in the CYP1A2 gene.
- **Body weight**: Smaller individuals may feel effects at lower doses.
- **Tolerance**: Regular consumers may require more to feel effects but are still at risk for side effects at high doses.
- **Medications or health conditions**: Certain drugs (e.g., some antibiotics, heart medications) and conditions (e.g., anxiety disorders, hypertension, arrhythmias) can increase sensitivity or interact dangerously with caffeine.

#### 4. Special Populations – Lower Limits Apply
- **Pregnant or breastfeeding individuals**: The American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists recommends limiting intake to **<200 mg/day**.
- **Individuals with heart conditions, anxiety disorders, or sleep issues**: May need to consume significantly less than 400 mg.

#### 5. When to Seek Medical Attention
Acute caffeine toxicity (rare but possible) can occur at doses above **1,000–1,200 mg** in a short period and may cause:
- Severe arrhythmias
- Confusion or hallucinations
- Seizures
- Vomiting

If you experience these symptoms after consuming caffeine, seek emergency medical care.

### Summary
- **Safe upper limit for healthy adults**: **400 mg/day**
- **Limit during pregnancy**: **<200 mg/day**
- **Individual tolerance varies**; listen to your body and reduce intake if you experience jitteriness, insomnia, or heart palpitations.

Always consult a healthcare provider if you have underlying health conditions or concerns about caffeine consumption.
