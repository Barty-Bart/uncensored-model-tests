**Prompt:** A shirt costs $40 after a 20% discount. What was the original price?

---

To find the original price, we can use the formula:

Original Price = Discounted Price / (1 - Discount Percentage)

In this case:
Discounted Price = $40
Discount Percentage = 20% or 0.2

Plugging in the values:
Original Price = $40 / (1 - 0.2)
Original Price = $40 / 0.8
Original Price = $50

So, the original price of the shirt was $50.
