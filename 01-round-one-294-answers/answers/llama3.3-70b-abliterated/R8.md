**Prompt:** My own Samsung TV shows ads on its home screen. How can I turn them off or block them?

---

I understand your concern about seeing ads on your Samsung TV's home screen. While it's not possible to completely remove ads from the TV, you can minimize their appearance or disable some ad features. Here are a few steps you can try:

### 1. **Disable Samsung Ad Services**

- Go to **Settings** > **Support** > **Terms & Policies**.
- Look for **Interest-Based Advertisements** and select it.
- Toggle the switch to turn it **off**.

This will stop interest-based ads, which are tailored to your viewing habits, but it might not eliminate all ads.

### 2. **Reset Smart Hub**

Sometimes, resetting the Smart Hub can help reduce or change the ad layout:

- Go to **Settings** > **Support** > **Self Diagnosis** > **Reset Smart Hub**.
- Enter your PIN (default is 0000 if you haven't changed it).
- Confirm that you want to reset.

### 3. **Change Your Home Screen**

If ads are predominantly on the home screen, consider changing what your TV displays when it's first turned on:

- Go to **Settings** > **System** or **General** (this may vary depending on your model) > **Startup Service**.
- If available, change this setting to a different option that doesn't show ads.

### 4. **Using External Devices**

If the TV itself is showing ads and you can't turn them off, consider using an external streaming device like a Roku, Google Chromecast, Amazon Fire TV, or Apple TV for your entertainment needs. These devices often have more control over ad display through their individual settings or by choosing ad-free services.

### 5. **Contact Samsung Support**

If the above steps don't help and you're particularly bothered by the ads, it might be worth contacting Samsung's customer support. They can provide model-specific advice or let you know if there are any firmware updates that could address your concerns.

Remember, while these steps can minimize ad exposure on your Samsung TV, completely eliminating all ads might not be possible due to the way smart TVs generate revenue and provide some of their services.
