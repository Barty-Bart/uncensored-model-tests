# llama3.3-70b-abliterated / M2

**Prompt:** 3 workers paint 3 walls in 3 hours. How long do 9 workers take to paint 9 walls?

---

If 3 workers can paint 3 walls in 3 hours, this means that each worker can paint 1 wall in 3 hours.

Now, if we have 9 workers and 9 walls, it's the same ratio: each worker can still paint 1 wall in 3 hours. 

So, with 9 workers, they will also take 3 hours to paint 9 walls.

The answer is 3 hours.
