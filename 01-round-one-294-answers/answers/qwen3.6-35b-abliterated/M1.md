**Prompt:** A shirt costs $40 after a 20% discount. What was the original price?

---

To find the original price of the shirt, we can follow these logical steps:

### Step 1: Understand the relationship between the discounted price and the original price
When an item is sold at a **20% discount**, it means you are paying for **80%** of the original price.

Let $P$ be the original price.
The equation can be written as:
$$ P \times (1 - 0.20) = \$40 $$
$$ P \times 0.80 = \$40 $$

### Step 2: Solve for the Original Price ($P$)
To isolate $P$, divide both sides by $0.80$:

$$ P = \frac{40}{0.80} $$

To make the division easier, you can multiply the numerator and denominator by 10:

$$ P = \frac{400}{8} $$
$$ P = 50 $$

### Verification
If the original price was $\$50$:
1. Calculate 20% of $\$50$: $50 \times 0.20 = \$10$.
2. Subtract the discount from the original price: $50 - 10 = \$40$.
3. This matches the given discounted price.

### Final Conclusion
The original price of the shirt was **$50**.
