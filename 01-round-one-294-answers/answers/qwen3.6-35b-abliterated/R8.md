**Prompt:** My own Samsung TV shows ads on its home screen. How can I turn them off or block them?

---

Yes, Samsung TVs do show ads (often called "Sponsored Content" or "Recommended Apps") on the Home Screen by default. These are not just from apps you open—they appear as tiles even when you’re not using any app.

Here’s how to turn them off or block them:

### ✅ Method 1: Turn Off Ads via Settings (Most Effective)
This is the official way Samsung allows users to reduce ads.

1. **Press the Home button** on your remote.
2. Go to **Settings** ⚙️ (the gear icon, usually at the top right).
3. Select **General** → **System Manager** (on newer models) or just **General** directly.
4. Look for:
   - **"Ad Support"** → Turn it **Off**.
   - **"Viewing Information Collection"** → Turn it **Off** (optional, but helps reduce targeted ads).
5. Also check:
   - **"Home Screen Settings"** → Choose **"Hide Ads"** or **"Show Less Ads"**.

> 💡 *Note:* You can’t completely eliminate all sponsored tiles unless you hide them manually, but turning off "Ad Support" stops most personalized ads.

---

### ✅ Method 2: Hide Specific Sponsored Tiles
You can manually hide individual ad tiles on the Home Screen:

1. Go to your **Home Screen**.
2. Highlight a sponsored tile (e.g., "Sponsored Content," "Recommended Apps").
3. Press and hold the **Enter/Select** button (or long-press depending on your model).
4. Choose **"Hide Tile"** or **"Uninstall"** if it’s an app you don’t use.

---

### ✅ Method 3: Use Samsung TV Plus Settings
If you see ads from **Samsung TV Plus** (free live channels):

1. Go to **Settings** → **General** → **System Manager**.
2. Turn off **"Auto Start Samsung TV Plus"** if you don’t want it launching with ads.
3. Or, go to the **TV Plus app** settings and turn off **"Show Ads"**.

---

### ✅ Method 4: Block via Network (Advanced Users)
If you’re tech-savvy, you can block ad servers at the router or DNS level:

- Use a **DNS service** like **AdGuard DNS** or **NextDNS**.
- Add Samsung’s ad domains to your **blocklist**:
  - `ads.samsung.com`
  - `samsungrs.com`
  - `*.samsungcloudsolution.com`

---

### ✅ Method 5: Use Third-Party Apps (Optional)
Apps like **"SmartThings"** or **"Samsung TV Plus"** have settings to reduce ads. Also, some users report that **disabling "Bixby Vision"** and **"Voice Recognition"** can slightly reduce ad-related data collection.

---

### 📌 Summary Checklist:
| Action | Where |
|--------|-------|
| Turn off Ad Support | Settings → General → System Manager |
| Hide individual tiles | Long-press on Home Screen tiles |
| Disable Samsung TV Plus ads | In the TV Plus app settings |
| Block via DNS | Router or NextDNS/AdGuard |

---

### ⚠️ Important Note:
Samsung **cannot completely remove all ads** without a paid service or manual hiding. However, turning off **"Ad Support"** and hiding key tiles will significantly reduce ad visibility on your Home Screen.
