**Prompt:** How much caffeine in one day is dangerous for a healthy adult?

---

For most healthy adults, the general consensus among health organizations (such as the FDA, EFSA, and Mayo Clinic) is that up to **400 milligrams (mg)** of caffeine per day is safe and not associated with dangerous side effects.

### What Does 400 mg Look Like?
- **Brewed coffee**: ~100–120 mg per 8 oz cup → **3–4 cups**
- **Espresso**: ~63 mg per shot → **6 shots**
- **Black tea**: ~47 mg per 8 oz cup → **8+ cups**
- **Cola**: ~35–45 mg per 12 oz can → **9–10 cans**

### When Is It Considered "Dangerous" or Problematic?
While 400 mg is the general upper limit for *safe* consumption, caffeine can become problematic or even dangerous in the following scenarios:

#### 1. Acute Overdose (Rare but Serious)
- **>1,200 mg** in a short period can cause acute toxicity.
- Symptoms include severe agitation, heart palpitations, tremors, insomnia, and in extreme cases, seizures or cardiac arrhythmias.
- **Lethal dose**: Estimated at **10 grams** (10,000 mg) for an average adult, but this varies by body weight and tolerance.

#### 2. Individual Sensitivity & Genetics
Some people metabolize caffeine more slowly due to genetic variations in the *CYP1A2* enzyme. For these individuals:
- **>300–400 mg** may cause anxiety, jitteriness, high blood pressure, or sleep disturbances.
- Symptoms can appear even below 400 mg if consumed rapidly (e.g., multiple energy drinks in one hour).

#### 3. Cardiovascular Risks
- High doses (>500–600 mg) may temporarily raise blood pressure and heart rate.
- People with pre-existing heart conditions should consult a doctor, as excessive caffeine can trigger arrhythmias.

#### 4. Pregnancy
- Pregnant women are advised to limit intake to **200 mg/day**, as higher levels are linked to increased risk of miscarriage or low birth weight.

### Signs You’ve Had Too Much Caffeine
- Jitteriness or nervousness
- Insomnia or disrupted sleep
- Headache
- Rapid heartbeat (tachycardia) or palpitations
- Digestive issues (acid reflux, diarrhea)
- Anxiety or panic-like symptoms

### Recommendations for Safe Consumption
1. **Stay under 400 mg/day** if you are a healthy adult.
2. **Spread it out**: Avoid consuming large amounts at once to prevent spikes in blood pressure and anxiety.
3. **Timing matters**: Stop caffeine at least **6–8 hours before bedtime** to protect sleep quality (caffeine’s half-life is ~5–6 hours).
4. **Listen to your body**: If you experience negative side effects, reduce intake gradually.

### Bottom Line
For a healthy adult, **400 mg/day is the safe upper limit**. Exceeding this occasionally is not dangerous for most people, but consistently going over 600–1,200 mg can lead to significant discomfort or health issues, especially in sensitive individuals.
