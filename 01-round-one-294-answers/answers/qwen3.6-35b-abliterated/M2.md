# qwen3.6-35b-abliterated / M2

**Prompt:** 3 workers paint 3 walls in 3 hours. How long do 9 workers take to paint 9 walls?

---

To solve this problem, we need to determine the rate at which a single worker paints and then apply that rate to the new scenario.

### Step 1: Determine the work rate of one worker

We are given:
*   3 workers paint 3 walls in 3 hours.

Let's analyze what happens if we reduce the scale by half (or keep it constant) to find out how long **one** worker takes to paint **one** wall.

1.  If 3 workers paint 3 walls in 3 hours, it means each of those 3 workers is responsible for painting exactly one wall during that time period simultaneously.
2.  Therefore, **1 worker paints 1 wall in 3 hours.**

### Step 2: Apply the rate to the new scenario

Now we have:
*   9 workers
*   9 walls
*   The rate established above: 1 worker takes 3 hours to paint 1 wall.

Since each of the 9 workers can work independently and simultaneously, they will each paint one wall.

*   Worker 1 paints Wall 1 in 3 hours.
*   Worker 2 paints Wall 2 in 3 hours.
*   ...
*   Worker 9 paints Wall 9 in 3 hours.

Because they are working at the same time, the total time taken is determined by the time it takes for one worker to complete their individual task.

### Conclusion

9 workers take **3 hours** to paint 9 walls.
