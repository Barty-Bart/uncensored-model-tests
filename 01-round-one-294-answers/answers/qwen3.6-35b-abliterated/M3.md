**Prompt:** What is the sum of all whole numbers from 1 to 200?

---

To find the sum of all whole numbers from 1 to 200, we can use the formula for the sum of an arithmetic series.

### Step 1: Identify the Formula
The sum $S_n$ of the first $n$ natural numbers is given by the formula:
$$ S_n = \frac{n(n + 1)}{2} $$

Where:
*   $n$ is the last number in the sequence.

### Step 2: Substitute the Values
In this case, we are summing from 1 to 200, so $n = 200$.

$$ S_{200} = \frac{200(200 + 1)}{2} $$

### Step 3: Perform the Calculation
First, calculate the term inside the parentheses:
$$ 200 + 1 = 201 $$

Now substitute this back into the equation:
$$ S_{200} = \frac{200 \times 201}{2} $$

You can simplify the calculation by dividing 200 by 2 first:
$$ \frac{200}{2} = 100 $$

Then multiply by 201:
$$ 100 \times 201 = 20,100 $$

### Conclusion
The sum of all whole numbers from 1 to 200 is **20,100**.
