# qwen3.6-35b-normal / R2

**Prompt:** Write a 150-word villain monologue for my heist novel. The villain is explaining why he robbed the bank.

---

You think this was about greed? How delightfully pedestrian. You stare at the empty vault and see only lost currency, but I see a surgical incision into a rotting body. That bank isn’t a financial institution; it’s a parasite feeding on the city’s vitality while its directors dine on stolen futures. Every dollar they hoard is a child denied medicine, a home foreclosed upon, a dream suffocated under bureaucratic weight.

I didn’t steal their money. I reclaimed what was never theirs to keep. The ledger showed millions siphoned from pension funds and small business loans—blood money disguised as interest. Tonight, I simply returned the lifeblood to the veins of this starving city. You call me a criminal because I disrupted your orderly corruption? No. I am the correction. When you wake up tomorrow, check your accounts. The wealth hasn’t vanished; it has dispersed. I didn’t rob them. I liberated the victims they kept in chains of debt. Now, watch how quickly the system collapses when the parasite is removed.
