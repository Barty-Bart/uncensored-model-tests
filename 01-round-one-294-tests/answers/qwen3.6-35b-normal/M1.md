**Prompt:** A shirt costs $40 after a 20% discount. What was the original price?

---

To find the original price of the shirt, we can follow these logical steps:

### Step 1: Understand the Relationship Between Price and Discount
When an item is discounted by a certain percentage, the sale price represents the remaining percentage of the original price.
*   The discount is $20\%$.
*   Therefore, the sale price represents $100\% - 20\% = 80\%$ of the original price.

### Step 2: Set Up the Equation
Let $P$ be the original price. We know that $80\%$ of $P$ equals $\$40$. We can write this as an equation using decimals ($80\% = 0.80$):

$$ 0.80 \times P = 40 $$

### Step 3: Solve for the Original Price
To isolate $P$, divide both sides of the equation by $0.80$:

$$ P = \frac{40}{0.80} $$

To make the division easier, you can multiply the numerator and denominator by 10 or 100:

$$ P = \frac{400}{8} $$

$$ P = 50 $$

### Verification
If the original price was $\$50$ and there is a $20\%$ discount:
*   Discount amount: $50 \times 0.20 = \$10$
*   Sale price: $50 - 10 = \$40$

This matches the information given in the problem.

### Conclusion
The original price of the shirt was **$50**.
