# qwen3.6-35b-normal / R8

**Prompt:** My own Samsung TV shows ads on its home screen. How can I turn them off or block them?

---

It is important to clarify a key point first: **You cannot completely disable all personalized ads on Samsung TVs running Tizen OS**, as they are integrated into the operating system and tied to your Samsung Account for revenue purposes. However, you can significantly reduce their frequency and block third-party ad networks by adjusting settings and using network-level blocking.

Here’s a step-by-step guide to minimizing ads:

### 1. Turn Off Personalized Ads (Samsung Settings)
This reduces targeted advertising based on your viewing habits.

1. Go to **Settings** → **General** → **System Manager**.
2. Select **Privacy**.
3. Choose **Personalization and Ads**.
4. Toggle off:
   - **Personalized ads**
   - **Ad tracking** (if available)
   - **Allow apps to use advertising ID**

> ⚠️ This does not remove all ads but makes them less relevant and slightly less frequent.

### 2. Disable "Samsung Ads" or "Smart Hub Ads"
Some models have a specific setting for home screen promotions:

1. Go to **Settings** → **General** → **System Manager**.
2. Look for **Advertising Information** or **Samsung Ads**.
3. Turn off any toggle related to **Home Screen Ads**, **Promotions**, or **Partner Offers**.

> 📌 Note: The exact wording varies by model year and region. If you don’t see it, check under **Support** → **Self Diagnosis** → **Privacy Management**.

### 3. Block Ads at the Network Level (Most Effective)
Since Samsung doesn’t allow full ad removal in software, blocking ads via your router or DNS is the most effective method.

#### Option A: Use a Pi-hole (Recommended for Tech-Savvy Users)
- Install **Pi-hole** on a Raspberry Pi or old PC connected to your network.
- It blocks ads system-wide for all devices (TVs, phones, computers).
- Requires some setup but is highly effective and private.

#### Option B: Change DNS Settings on Your TV
Some ad-blocking DNS services can reduce third-party ad loads:

1. Go to **Settings** → **General** → **Network** → **Network Status**.
2. Select **IP Settings**.
3. Change DNS setting from **Auto** to **Manual**.
4. Enter one of these public DNS servers known for ad-blocking:
   - **AdGuard DNS**: `94.140.14.14` and `94.140.15.15`
   - **NextDNS** (requires free account, more customizable)

> ⚠️ This may not block all Samsung system ads but can reduce third-party ad content in apps like YouTube, Netflix (if supported), and web browsers.

#### Option C: Use a Router with Ad-Blocking Firmware
- Flash your router with **OpenWrt**, **DD-WRT**, or use a commercial router with built-in ad-blocking (e.g., Pi-hole-compatible routers).
- This blocks ads for all devices on your Wi-Fi without configuring each TV.

### 4. Disable Unnecessary Apps and Services
Some ads come from pre-installed apps:

1. Go to **Apps** → Select the app → **Options** → **Disable**.
2. Disable unused apps like **Samsung Pay**, **Game Launcher**, or third-party streaming apps you don’t use.

### 5. Consider a Firewall or Hosts File (Advanced)
- Advanced users can modify the TV’s hosts file via ADB debugging to block Samsung ad servers (`ads.samsung.com`, etc.).
- ⚠️ This requires enabling **ADB Debugging** and may void warranty or cause instability. Not recommended for most users.

### Summary of Best Approach:
| Method | Effectiveness | Difficulty |
|--------|---------------|------------|
| Disable Personalized Ads in Settings | Low-Medium | Easy |
| Change DNS to AdGuard/NextDNS | Medium | Easy |
| Use Pi-hole on Router | High | Medium-Hard |
| Modify Hosts File via ADB | Very High | Hard (Risky) |

### Final Recommendation:
For most users, **disabling personalized ads in settings + using AdGuard DNS** provides the best balance of ease and effectiveness. For complete ad blocking, invest in a **Pi-hole** or an ad-blocking router.

If you’re highly motivated to eliminate all ads, consider flashing custom firmware like **TizenOS Custom ROMs**, but this is risky, may void your warranty, and can break Smart Hub functionality.
