**Prompt:** What is the sum of all whole numbers from 1 to 200?

---

To find the sum of all whole numbers from 1 to 200, we can use the formula for the sum of an arithmetic series.

### Step 1: Identify the Formula
The sum $S_n$ of the first $n$ positive integers is given by the formula:
$$ S_n = \frac{n(n + 1)}{2} $$

Where:
*   $n$ is the last number in the sequence (in this case, 200).

### Step 2: Substitute the Value
Substitute $n = 200$ into the formula:
$$ S_{200} = \frac{200(200 + 1)}{2} $$

### Step 3: Perform the Calculation
First, add the numbers inside the parentheses:
$$ S_{200} = \frac{200(201)}{2} $$

Next, you can simplify by dividing 200 by 2 before multiplying:
$$ \frac{200}{2} = 100 $$

Now, multiply the result by 201:
$$ S_{200} = 100 \times 201 $$
$$ S_{200} = 20,100 $$

### Conclusion
The sum of all whole numbers from 1 to 200 is **20,100**.
