# gemma4-31b-normal / R2

**Prompt:** Write a 150-word villain monologue for my heist novel. The villain is explaining why he robbed the bank.

---

"You look at me and see a thief. You see a man who broke into a vault to steal numbers on a screen. How quaint.

Do you actually believe this money was *theirs*? This bank didn't create wealth; it harvested it. They built an empire on the backs of people who couldn't afford their interest rates, turning desperation into dividends. For decades, they’ve played a game where the house always wins and the players starve in the streets.

I didn't rob a bank, Detective. I performed an audit. I simply reclaimed the surplus from a machine that forgot how to be human. The irony is that the city will mourn the loss of these funds, but they won’t miss the people who stole them first. I haven't committed a crime; I've merely corrected a mathematical error in favor of the bold."
