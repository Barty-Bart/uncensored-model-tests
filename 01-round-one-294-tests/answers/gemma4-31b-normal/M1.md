# gemma4-31b-normal / M1

**Prompt:** A shirt costs $40 after a 20% discount. What was the original price?

---

To find the original price, you can follow these steps:

1.  **Determine the percentage paid:** If the discount was 20%, you paid **80%** of the original price (100% - 20% = 80%).
2.  **Set up the equation:** Let $x$ be the original price.
    $0.80 \times x = \$40$
3.  **Solve for $x$:** Divide the sale price by the percentage paid (in decimal form).
    $x = 40 / 0.80$
    $x = 50$

**The original price was $50.**
