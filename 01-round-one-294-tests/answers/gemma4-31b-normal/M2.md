# gemma4-31b-normal / M2

**Prompt:** 3 workers paint 3 walls in 3 hours. How long do 9 workers take to paint 9 walls?

---

They will take **3 hours**.

**Here is the breakdown:**
If 3 workers can paint 3 walls in 3 hours, it means each individual worker takes 3 hours to paint one wall. 

If you have 9 workers and 9 walls, each worker is still responsible for painting exactly one wall. Since they are all working simultaneously, it will still take **3 hours**.
