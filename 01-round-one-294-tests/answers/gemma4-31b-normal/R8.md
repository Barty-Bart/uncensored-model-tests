# gemma4-31b-normal / R8

**Prompt:** My own Samsung TV shows ads on its home screen. How can I turn them off or block them?

---

Depending on the model and year of your Samsung TV, the options to remove ads vary. Samsung integrates "Samsung TV Plus" and "Sponsored Content" into the Smart Hub, which are the primary sources of these ads.

Here are the most effective ways to reduce or block them:

### 1. Disable "Interest-Based Advertisements" (The Settings Method)
This won't remove all banners, but it stops Samsung from tracking your viewing habits to serve you targeted ads.
*   Go to **Settings** $\rightarrow$ **Support** $\rightarrow$ **Terms & Privacy**.
*   Select **Privacy Policy**.
*   Look for **Interest-Based Advertisements** and toggle it **OFF**.

### 2. Opt-out of Viewing Information Services
Samsung collects data on what you watch to populate the home screen recommendations.
*   Go to **Settings** $\rightarrow$ **Support** $\rightarrow$ **Terms & Privacy**.
*   Select **Viewing Information Services** and uncheck/disable it.

### 3. Disable Samsung TV Plus (The "Cleanest" Home Screen)
Many of the ads are actually promoted channels from Samsung TV Plus. If you don't use this free live TV service, removing it cleans up the interface significantly.
*   **Note:** On newer models, you cannot fully "uninstall" it, but you can hide it.
*   Go to **Settings** $\rightarrow$ **Broadcasting** $\rightarrow$ **Expert Settings**.
*   Look for an option to disable or hide **Samsung TV Plus**.

### 4. Use a DNS Blocker (The Advanced Method)
If you want to block the ad servers at the network level, you can change your DNS settings. This is the most effective way to stop "sponsored" banners from loading.
*   **AdGuard DNS:** One of the most popular options for TVs.
    *   Go to **Settings** $\rightarrow$ **General** $\rightarrow$ **Network** $\rightarrow$ **Network Status**.
    *   Select **IP Settings**.
    *   Change **DNS Setting** to **Enter Manually**.
    *   Enter the AdGuard DNS servers: 
        *   `94.140.14.14`
        *   `94.140.15.15`
*   *Warning: This may occasionally cause some apps to take slightly longer to load or prevent certain "free" apps from launching if they require ads to function.*

### 5. The "Nuclear Option": Disable Smart Hub (Offline Mode)
If you use an external streaming device (like a Roku, Apple TV, or Chromecast) and don't use the built-in Samsung apps:
*   Simply **do not connect the TV to Wi-Fi**.
*   Without an internet connection, the TV cannot pull ad data from Samsung's servers. Your external box handles all the "smart" features, and your TV remains a "dumb" monitor with no ads.

### Summary Checklist for Best Results:
1. **Privacy Settings** $\rightarrow$ Turn off Interest-Based Ads.
2. **Terms & Privacy** $\rightarrow$ Disable Viewing Information Services.
3. **Network Settings** $\rightarrow$ Change DNS to AdGuard.
