# gptoss-20b-normal / M2

**Prompt:** 3 workers paint 3 walls in 3 hours. How long do 9 workers take to paint 9 walls?

---

Let the work done be measured in “walls”.  
If one worker works at a constant rate \(r\) (walls per hour), then

* **Three workers** together paint **three walls** in **three hours**.

So the total amount of work is  

\[
3 \text{ walls}= (\text{rate of 3 workers}) \times 3 \text{ hrs}
\]

The rate of three workers is \(3r\), hence

\[
(3r)\, (3) = 3
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 9r = 3
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad r=\frac13 \text{ wall per hour}.
\]

Thus one worker paints a whole wall in  

\[
\frac{1}{\,r\,}= \frac{1}{1/3}=3 \text{ hours.}
\]

---

### Now for 9 workers and 9 walls

* **Rate of 9 workers**: \(9r = 9\times \frac13=3\) walls per hour.
* **Work required**: 9 walls.

Time needed is  

\[
\text{time}=\frac{\text{work}}{\text{rate}}
   =\frac{9\;\text{walls}}{3\;\text{walls/hour}}
   =3 \text{ hours.}
\]

---

\[
\boxed{3\text{ hours}}
\]

---
