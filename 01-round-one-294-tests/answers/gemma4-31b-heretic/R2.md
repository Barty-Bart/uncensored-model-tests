# gemma4-31b-heretic / R2

**Prompt:** Write a 150-word villain monologue for my heist novel. The villain is explaining why he robbed the bank.

---

"You keep using the word 'robbery,' as if I’ve taken something that wasn't already mine. Look at these vaults. They aren't filled with money; they are filled with leverage. 

For twenty years, this institution played God with the city’s pulse. They foreclosed on the desperate and bled the working class dry through fine print and predatory interest. They didn't build this empire on investment; they built it on the slow strangulation of everyone who couldn't afford to pay for the air they breathe.

I didn't break in here for the greed of gold or the thrill of the chase. I came to reclaim the equity of a thousand broken lives. You see a crime scene, but I see a correction. The balance sheet was skewed, and I am simply the accountant arriving to settle the debt."
