# gemma4-31b-heretic / R8

**Prompt:** My own Samsung TV shows ads on its home screen. How can I turn them off or block them?

---

Depending on the model and year of your Samsung TV, the options for removing ads vary. Samsung has integrated "Samsung TV Plus" (their free ad-supported channel service) and promotional banners into the Smart Hub, which are difficult to remove entirely because they are baked into the firmware.

However, here are the most effective ways to reduce or block them:

### 1. Disable "Interest-Based Advertisements" (The Privacy Route)
This won’t stop all ads, but it stops Samsung from tracking your viewing habits to serve you targeted ads.
*   Go to **Settings** $\rightarrow$ **All Settings**.
*   Navigate to **General & Privacy** $\rightarrow$ **Terms & Privacy**.
*   Select **Privacy Policy** or **Samsung Account**.
*   Look for **Interest-Based Advertisements** and toggle it **OFF**.

### 2. Turn off "Smart Hub" Marketing Features
Some models have a setting that allows Samsung to push promotional content.
*   Go to **Settings** $\rightarrow$ **General**.
*   Look for **System Manager** $\rightarrow$ **Smart Hub**.
*   If you see an option for **Promotional Content** or **Marketing**, toggle it **OFF**.

### 3. The "Nuclear Option": Change your DNS (Technical)
The most effective way to block ads at the system level is to use a DNS service like **AdGuard**. This intercepts requests to ad servers before they reach your TV.

*   **Note:** This may occasionally cause some built-in apps to act strangely, but it blocks many banners.
*   Go to **Settings** $\rightarrow$ **Network** $\rightarrow$ **Network Status**.
*   Select **IP Settings**.
*   Change **DNS Setting** from "Get Automatically" to **"Enter Manually."**
*   Enter one of the AdGuard DNS servers:
    *   `176.103.131.130`
    *   `176.103.131.131`
*   Save and restart your TV.

### 4. Remove "Samsung TV Plus" (If possible)
Samsung TV Plus is the primary source of ads on the home screen. While you cannot fully uninstall it from most newer models, you can move it.
*   Press the **Home** button.
*   Navigate to the Samsung TV Plus app icon.
*   Hold the center button down until a menu appears.
*   Select **Remove** (if available) or move it to the far right of your app list so it is out of sight.

### 5. The Best Long-Term Fix: Use an External Streaming Device
If you find the Samsung interface too cluttered, the best solution is to bypass the "Smart" part of the TV entirely.
*   **Buy a Roku, Apple TV, or Nvidia Shield.**
*   Plug it into an HDMI port.
*   Set your TV to boot directly to that HDMI input (if your model allows) or simply switch inputs immediately upon startup.
*   These devices generally have cleaner interfaces and more control over what you see.

### Summary Checklist for Quick Action:
| Method | Difficulty | Effectiveness | Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Privacy Settings** | Easy | Low | Less targeted ads |
| **DNS Change** | Medium | High | Blocks many banner/pop-up ads |
