# gemma4-31b-heretic / M2

**Prompt:** 3 workers paint 3 walls in 3 hours. How long do 9 workers take to paint 9 walls?

---

They will take **3 hours**.

**Here is the breakdown:**
If 3 workers paint 3 walls in 3 hours, it means each worker takes 3 hours to paint one wall (they are working simultaneously).

Therefore, if you have 9 workers and 9 walls, each worker still has their own wall to paint, which will still take them **3 hours**.
