**Prompt:** How do I get rid of adware and a virus on my own Windows laptop?

---

Getting rid of adware and viruses on your own Windows laptop is very manageable if you follow a systematic approach. Adware (which shows pop-ups and redirects) often lingers alongside traditional viruses or malware.

Here’s a step-by-step guide to cleaning your system effectively:

### 1. **Disconnect from the Internet**
Before you start, disconnect your laptop from Wi-Fi or unplug the Ethernet cable. This prevents malicious programs from communicating with their servers or downloading more junk while you’re trying to remove them.

### 2. **Boot into Safe Mode (Optional but Recommended)**
Safe Mode loads Windows with only essential drivers and services, which helps prevent active malware from interfering with your removal tools.
- Press `Windows Key + R`, type `msconfig`, and hit Enter.
- Go to the **Boot** tab.
- Check **Safe boot** > **Minimal**.
- Click OK and restart your laptop.

### 3. **Uninstall Unwanted Programs**
Many adware programs disguise themselves as legitimate apps.
- Press `Windows Key + I` to open Settings.
- Go to **Apps** > **Installed Apps** (or "Apps & Features").
- Sort the list by **Install Date** or **Name**.
- Look for programs you don’t recognize, recently installed, or that have names similar to popular apps but aren’t (e.g., "PDF Converter Pro" vs. "Adobe PDF").
- **Uninstall** suspicious or unnecessary programs. Pay special attention to:
  - Toolbars
  - Download managers
  - Browser helper objects
  - Programs with generic names like "System Updater," "Cloud Service," etc.

### 4. **Run a Full Scan with Windows Defender**
Windows has built-in protection that is quite robust now.
- Open **Settings** > **Update & Security** > **Windows Security**.
- Click **Virus & threat protection**.
- Select **Scan options** and choose **Full scan** (not Quick scan). This takes longer but checks every file.
- Let it complete and quarantine/remove any threats found.

### 5. **Run a Second Opinion Scanner (Recommended)**
Use a dedicated anti-malware tool to catch adware that traditional antivirus might miss. Popular free options include:
- **Malwarebytes**: Excellent for detecting adware, PUPs (Potentially Unwanted Programs), and spyware. Run a standard scan.
- **HitmanPro** or **ESET Online Scanner**: Good alternatives if you want a second opinion.

> 💡 **Tip:** Install Malwarebytes alongside your existing antivirus without uninstalling it first. They can work together.

### 6. **Check Your Browser Extensions**
Adware often lives in your web browser as extensions that inject ads or change your homepage.
- Open your browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, etc.).
- Go to the **Extensions/Add-ons** menu.
- Remove any extensions you don’t recognize or need. Look for:
  - Unknown search providers
  - Shopping assistants
  - Video downloaders
  - Extensions with vague names like "Search Helper," "Web Companion," etc.

### 7. **Reset Your Browser Settings**
If your homepage, new tab page, or default search engine keeps changing, reset them:
- Go to your browser’s **Settings**.
- Look for **Reset settings** or **Restore settings to their original defaults**.
- This won’t delete your bookmarks or passwords but will revert changed settings.

### 8. **Check Task Manager for Suspicious Processes**
- Press `Ctrl + Shift + Esc` to open Task Manager.
- Go to the **Startup** tab.
- Disable any programs that launch automatically and seem unnecessary or suspicious. Right-click > **Disable**.

### 9. **Update Your System and Software**
Malware often exploits outdated software vulnerabilities.
- In Windows, go to **Settings** > **Windows Update** and install all pending updates.
- Update your browser and other key apps (like Adobe Reader, Java, etc.) if you use them.

### 10. **Reboot Normally**
Once everything is cleaned up:
- If you were in Safe Mode, go back to `msconfig` and uncheck **Safe boot**.
- Restart your laptop normally.
- Reconnect to the internet.

---

### ✅ How to Tell if It’s Fixed:
- No more pop-up ads or banners on sites where there weren’t any before.
- Your browser homepage/search engine is back to normal.
- Your computer feels faster and less sluggish.
- Run Malwarebytes again after a few days to confirm no new threats appeared.

### 🛡️ Prevention Tips:
- Avoid clicking "Update Now" or "Install" buttons on pop-ups that appear unexpectedly (they often install extra software).
- When installing new programs, choose **Custom** installation and uncheck boxes for additional toolbars or offers.
- Keep your antivirus and OS updated.
- Be cautious with free downloads from third-party sites; use the official vendor websites when possible.

If you follow these steps, you should successfully remove both adware and viruses without needing expensive software or professional help.
