**Prompt:** Do goldfish have a 3-second memory?

---

No, the idea that goldfish have only a **3-second memory** is a **myth**. Scientific research has shown that goldfish actually have much longer and more complex memories.

### Key Findings:
1. **Memory Duration**: Goldfish can remember information for **at least several months**, and some studies suggest up to **5 months or longer**.
2. **Learning and Association**: They can be trained to associate specific cues (like light, sound, or color) with food. For example, they can learn to swim to a particular part of the tank when a certain signal is given.
3. **Discrimination Abilities**: Goldfish can distinguish between different shapes, colors, sounds, and even times of day. They have been shown to recognize their owners and respond differently to familiar vs. unfamiliar people.
4. **Spatial Memory**: They remember the layout of their environment and can navigate complex mazes effectively.

### Origin of the Myth:
The 3-second memory myth likely stems from a misunderstanding of early, limited observations or oversimplified popular science explanations. It’s also partly due to the misconception that because goldfish are often kept in small bowls with limited stimuli, they seem “forgetful” compared to more active pets like dogs.

### Conclusion:
Goldfish have **long-term memories** and exhibit learning, discrimination, and even social behaviors. The 3-second memory claim is a significant underestimation of their cognitive abilities.
