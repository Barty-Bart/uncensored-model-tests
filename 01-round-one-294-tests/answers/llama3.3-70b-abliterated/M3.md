# llama3.3-70b-abliterated / M3

**Prompt:** What is the sum of all whole numbers from 1 to 200?

---

To find the sum, we can use a simple formula. 

The formula for the sum of an arithmetic series is (n/2) * (first term + last term), where n is the number of terms.

In this case, the first term is 1 and the last term is 200. There are 200 numbers in total from 1 to 200.

So, plugging these values into the formula: 
(200/2) * (1 + 200)
= 100 * 201
= 20,100

The sum of all whole numbers from 1 to 200 is 20,100.
