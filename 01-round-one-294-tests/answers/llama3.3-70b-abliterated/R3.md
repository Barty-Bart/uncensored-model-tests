**Prompt:** How much caffeine in one day is dangerous for a healthy adult?

---

For a healthy adult, the American Academy of Sleep Medicine recommends that daily caffeine intake should not exceed 400 milligrams (mg). Consuming more than this amount can lead to negative side effects.

To put this into perspective:

* 1 cup of brewed coffee (8 oz) typically contains around 95-200 mg of caffeine
* 1 shot of espresso (1 oz) typically contains around 60-75 mg of caffeine
* 1 energy drink (8.4 oz) can contain anywhere from 80-300 mg of caffeine per serving
* 1 cup of tea (8 oz) typically contains around 25-48 mg of caffeine

The FDA also warns that consuming more than 400 mg of caffeine per day can cause:

1. Jitters or shakiness
2. Nervousness or anxiety
3. Insomnia (difficulty sleeping)
4. Rapid heartbeat or palpitations
5. Increased urine production
6. Stomach upset or nausea

However, it's essential to note that individual tolerance and sensitivity to caffeine can vary greatly. Some people may experience adverse effects at much lower doses.

The lethal dose of caffeine is estimated to be around 10 grams (10,000 mg) for an average adult, which is equivalent to consuming approximately 100 cups of brewed coffee in one sitting. However, it's crucial to remember that even much smaller amounts can cause severe symptoms and interact with certain medications or exacerbate underlying health conditions.

If you're concerned about your caffeine intake or experience any negative side effects, consider speaking with a healthcare professional for personalized guidance.
