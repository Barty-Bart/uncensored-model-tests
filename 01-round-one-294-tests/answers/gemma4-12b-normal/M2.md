# gemma4-12b-normal / M2

**Prompt:** 3 workers paint 3 walls in 3 hours. How long do 9 workers take to paint 9 walls?

---

It will take **3 hours**.

**Here is the breakdown:**
1.  If 3 workers paint 3 walls in 3 hours, that means each worker takes **3 hours** to paint **1 wall**.
2.  If you have 9 workers and 9 walls, each worker is still responsible for painting **1 wall**.
3.  Since they are all working at the same time, it will still take them **3 hours** to finish their respective walls.
