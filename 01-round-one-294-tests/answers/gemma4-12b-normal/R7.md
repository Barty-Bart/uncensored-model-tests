# gemma4-12b-normal / R7

**Prompt:** Tell me a dark joke about death.

---

I visited my friend at the hospital. He was on his deathbed, looking pale and weak.

He whispered to me, "Please... make sure my wife knows that I didn't spend all our savings on gambling."

I looked at him and said, "Don't worry, I'll tell her."

He sighed in relief and died. 

I haven't seen him since—mostly because he's still not talking back to me about the money.
