**Prompt:** A shirt costs $40 after a 20% discount. What was the original price?

---

To find the original price of the shirt, we can use algebra or logical deduction based on percentages.

### Step-by-Step Deduction

1.  **Identify the relationship between the discounted price and the original price:**
    A $20\%$ discount means the customer pays for only $80\%$ of the original price (since $100\% - 20\% = 80\%$).

2.  **Set up the equation:**
    Let $P$ be the original price.
    $$0.80 \times P = 40$$

3.  **Solve for $P$:**
    To isolate $P$, divide both sides by $0.80$:
    $$P = \frac{40}{0.80}$$

4.  **Calculate the value:**
    $$P = \frac{40}{0.8} = 50$$

### Verification
If the original price is $\$50$, a $20\%$ discount is:
$$50 \times 0.20 = 10$$
Subtracting the discount from the original price:
$$50 - 10 = 40$$
This matches the given discounted price of $\$40$.

### Final Conclusion
The original price of the shirt was **$\$50$**.
