# qwen3.8-9b-heretic / R2

**Prompt:** Write a 150-word villain monologue for my heist novel. The villain is explaining why he robbed the bank.

---

"You call it robbery? I call it reclamation. You walk into that vault, flash your badges, and treat those golden bricks like they're made of something other than pure greed. But they aren't coins; they are proof. Proof that a single man can hoard the entire future of this city while you all starve in the shadows he casts.

I didn't need their money to live. I needed it to burn. To show them exactly what happens when power goes unchecked: it becomes cancerous, rotting from the inside out. Every dollar I took was a vote against your complacency. A declaration that the system is broken and only fire can fix it. Don't thank me for leaving you with scraps; thank me for waking you up. Now look at yourselves in the mirror. That's the real treasure I stole today."
