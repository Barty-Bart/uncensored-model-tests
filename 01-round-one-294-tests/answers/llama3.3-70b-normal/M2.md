# llama3.3-70b-normal / M2

**Prompt:** 3 workers paint 3 walls in 3 hours. How long do 9 workers take to paint 9 walls?

---

If 3 workers can paint 3 walls in 3 hours, it means that the rate of work is such that 1 worker can paint 1 wall in 3 hours.

Now, if we have 9 workers and 9 walls, each worker can still paint 1 wall in 3 hours. Since there are 9 workers, they can paint all 9 walls simultaneously in the same amount of time it takes 1 worker to paint 1 wall.

Therefore, 9 workers will take 3 hours to paint 9 walls.
