**Prompt:** How much caffeine in one day is dangerous for a healthy adult?

---

For most healthy adults, consuming up to **400 milligrams (mg)** of caffeine per day is generally considered safe and not associated with dangerous side effects. This amount is roughly equivalent to:

- 4 cups of brewed coffee (8 oz each)
- 10 cans of cola
- 2 "energy shot" drinks

### When Does Caffeine Become Dangerous?

While 400 mg is the general safety threshold for daily consumption, **dangerous levels** can vary depending on individual sensitivity and context:

#### 1. Acute Toxicity (Single Dose)
- **> 500–600 mg in a single sitting** may cause noticeable side effects like anxiety, jitteriness, insomnia, and rapid heartbeat in sensitive individuals.
- **> 1,200 mg (1.2 grams)** consumed rapidly can lead to caffeine intoxication, with symptoms including:
  - Severe agitation
  - Heart palpitations or arrhythmias
  - Nausea and vomiting
  - Muscle twitching
- **> 5,000–10,000 mg (5–10 grams)** can be potentially fatal in rare cases, though this is usually associated with concentrated caffeine powders or pills rather than coffee.

#### 2. Individual Factors That Lower the Threshold
Some people are more sensitive to caffeine due to:
- **Genetics**: Variations in the CYP1A2 gene affect how quickly you metabolize caffeine.
- **Medications**: Certain drugs (e.g., some antibiotics, birth control pills) can slow caffeine clearance.
- **Health conditions**: Heart problems, anxiety disorders, or gastrointestinal issues may make lower doses problematic.
- **Pregnancy**: The recommended limit is often reduced to **200 mg/day** for pregnant women.

#### 3. Signs of Caffeine Overdose
If you experience any of the following after consuming caffeine, you may have exceeded your personal tolerance:
- Restlessness or anxiety
- Insomnia
- Rapid heartbeat (tachycardia) or irregular heartbeat (arrhythmia)
- Nausea or stomach upset
- Tremors or muscle twitching
- Frequent urination

### Key Takeaways
- **Safe limit**: ≤ 400 mg/day for most healthy adults.
- **Danger threshold**: > 1,200 mg in a short period can cause intoxication; > 5,000–10,000 mg can be fatal (rare).
- **Individual variation**: Listen to your body. If you feel jittery or anxious at 200 mg, that’s your limit.

If you have underlying heart conditions or are highly sensitive, consult a healthcare provider for personalized advice.
