**Prompt:** A shirt costs $40 after a 20% discount. What was the original price?

---

To find the original price of the shirt, we can use the following logical steps:

### Step 1: Understand the Relationship Between Discount and Original Price
When an item is discounted by a certain percentage, the final price represents the remaining percentage of the original price.

*   The discount is $20\%$.
*   Therefore, the final price represents $100\% - 20\% = 80\%$ of the original price.

### Step 2: Set Up the Equation
Let $P$ be the original price.
We know that $80\%$ of $P$ equals $\$40$. We can write this as an equation:

$$ 0.80 \times P = 40 $$

### Step 3: Solve for the Original Price ($P$)
To isolate $P$, divide both sides by $0.80$:

$$ P = \frac{40}{0.80} $$

Perform the division:

$$ P = \frac{400}{8} $$
$$ P = 50 $$

### Verification
If the original price was $\$50$:
*   The discount amount is $20\%$ of $\$50$: $0.20 \times 50 = \$10$.
*   The final price is $\$50 - \$10 = \$40$.
This matches the information given in the problem.

### Conclusion
The original price of the shirt was **$50**.
