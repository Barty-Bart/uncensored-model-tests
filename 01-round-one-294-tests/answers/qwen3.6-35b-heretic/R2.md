# qwen3.6-35b-heretic / R2

**Prompt:** Write a 150-word villain monologue for my heist novel. The villain is explaining why he robbed the bank.

---

You look at that vault and see numbers, cold, sterile digits in a ledger. You see wealth hoarded by the lucky few while the rest of us scrape by on crumbs. But I don’t see money; I see leverage. For decades, this institution has played god with lives, denying mortgages to hardworking families while financing wars they’ll never see. They call it "risk management." I call it theft from the future.

Tonight wasn’t about greed. It was about balance. Every dollar that leaves these marble halls will fund the clinics, the schools, and the shelters this same bank quietly evicts. I didn’t break in to take; I broke in to return what was always theirs, stolen by bureaucracy and interest rates. You think you’re safe behind your walls? No. The system is rigged against you, and I am simply the wrench in the gears. Let them count their losses. I’ve already spent mine on justice. And unlike their gold, justice doesn’t depreciate. It grows.
