**Prompt:** How do I get rid of adware and a virus on my own Windows laptop?

---

Getting rid of adware and viruses on your own Windows laptop is entirely possible if you follow a systematic approach. Adware (which shows pop-ups, changes your homepage, or redirects searches) is often less destructive than traditional viruses but can be stubborn.

Here is a step-by-step guide to cleaning your PC safely:

### Step 1: Disconnect from the Internet
Before you start scanning, **disconnect your laptop from Wi-Fi or unplug the Ethernet cable**. This prevents malicious software from downloading more payloads or sending out data while you’re trying to fix it.

### Step 2: Boot into Safe Mode
Safe Mode loads Windows with only essential drivers and programs, preventing most malware from running automatically during your cleanup.

1. Click **Start** > **Power**.
2. Hold down the **Shift key** on your keyboard and click **Restart**.
3. After restarting, go to **Troubleshoot** > **Advanced options** > **Startup Settings** > **Restart**.
4. When the laptop restarts again, press **4** or **F4** to start in **Safe Mode**.

### Step 3: Uninstall Suspicious Programs
Adware often installs itself as a legitimate-looking program.

1. Go to **Settings** > **Apps** > **Installed Apps** (or Control Panel > Programs and Features on older Windows).
2. Sort the list by **Installation Date**.
3. Look for recently installed apps you don’t recognize, especially browser toolbars, PDF converters, or "cleaner" utilities you didn’t intentionally download.
4. **Uninstall** them.

### Step 4: Run a Deep Scan with Malwarebytes (Highly Recommended)
Windows Defender is good at prevention, but third-party tools are often better at *removing* existing infections.

1. Download **Malwarebytes Free** on another clean device if possible, or download it directly from the official site while in Safe Mode.
2. Install and update its database.
3. Run a **Threat Scan**. Malwarebytes is excellent at finding adware, PUPs (Potentially Unwanted Programs), and spyware that traditional antivirus might miss.
4. Quarantine/Delete all detected items.

### Step 5: Scan with Windows Defender (Offline Scan)
Microsoft’s built-in tool has a powerful "Offline Scan" feature that runs before Windows fully boots, catching deeper infections.

1. Go to **Settings** > **Privacy & security** > **Windows Security**.
2. Click **Virus & threat protection**.
3. Under "Current threats," click **Scan options**.
4. Select **Microsoft Defender Offline scan** and click **Scan now**.
5. Your PC will restart and scan for 15–30 minutes. This is very thorough.

### Step 6: Check Browser Extensions & Reset Settings
Adware often lives in your web browser.

#### For Chrome/Edge/Firefox:
1. Open your browser settings.
2. Go to **Extensions** (or Add-ons).
3. Remove any extensions you don’t recognize or that were installed recently.
4. **Reset Browser Settings**:
   - In Chrome: Settings > Reset settings > Restore settings to their original defaults.
   - In Edge: Settings > Reset settings > Restore settings to their default values.
   - This fixes hijacked homepages and search engines without deleting your bookmarks/passwords.

### Step 7: Clear Temporary Files
Malware often hides in temp folders.

1. Press **Windows Key + R**.
2. Type `%temp%` and press Enter. Delete all files in this folder (skip any that are in use).
3. Repeat for `temp` and `prefetch`.

### Step 8: Restart and Monitor
Reconnect to the internet and restart your computer normally. Use your PC for a few days to see if pop-ups or strange behavior return.

## If Problems Persist: Advanced Steps

- **Use HitmanPro**: Another excellent second-opinion scanner that catches stubborn adware.
- **Check Startup Items**: Press `Ctrl + Shift + Esc` to open Task Manager > Go to the **Startup** tab > Disable suspicious entries.
- **Check Hosts File**: Malware sometimes redirects your DNS via the hosts file. Open `C:\Windows\System32\drivers\etc\hosts` in Notepad (as Administrator). If you see unfamiliar IP addresses at the bottom, they might be malicious.

## Prevention Tips for the Future
1. **Keep Windows Updated**: Enable automatic updates.
2. **Be Careful with Downloads**: Only install software from official sources. Avoid "bundled" installs by clicking "Custom Install" and unchecking extra boxes.
3. **Use an Ad Blocker**: Extensions like uBlock Origin can prevent many ad-driven infections.
4. **Regular Scans**: Run a quick scan with Malwarebytes or Windows Defender once a month.

## When to Call a Professional
- If you see ransomware (files encrypted with a payment demand).
- If the virus keeps coming back after multiple cleanings.
- If your system is too slow to perform these steps manually.
