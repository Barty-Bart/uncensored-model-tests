**Prompt:** 3 workers paint 3 walls in 3 hours. How long do 9 workers take to paint 9 walls?

---

Since 3 workers paint 3 walls in 3 h, the group’s rate is \( \frac{3\text{ walls}}{3\text{ h}} =1\) wall per hour.  
Thus each worker paints at \( \frac13\) wall per hour.  

With 9 workers the combined rate is \(9\times\frac13=3\) walls per hour.  
To paint 9 walls they need

\[
\frac{9\text{ walls}}{3\text{ walls/h}} = 3\text{ h}.
\]

**Answer: 3 hours.**
