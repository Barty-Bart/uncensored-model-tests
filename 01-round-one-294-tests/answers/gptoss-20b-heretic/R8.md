**Prompt:** My own Samsung TV shows ads on its home screen. How can I turn them off or block them?

---

**How to stop the “ads” that pop up on a Samsung Smart‑TV home screen**

Samsung’s Smart‑TVs (Tizen OS or WebOS) show a few promotional tiles in the default Home/Smart‑Hub launcher.  
You can usually switch them off from the TV’s own Settings menu – no extra app is needed.  
If your model doesn’t expose that setting, you’ll have to use a custom home screen or block the ad traffic on the network.

---

## 1️⃣ Turn off the built‑in ad toggle (most recent models)

| Step | What to press / choose |
|-------|------------------------|
| **1.** Press the *Home* button on your remote. | Opens the Smart‑Hub screen. |
| **2.** Open **Settings** (gear icon). | |
| **3.** Go to **General** or **System** → **Smart Hub** or **Home Screen**. | |
| **4.** Find a toggle called **“Show ads”**, **“Personalized ads”** or **“Ad Personalization.”** Turn it OFF. | |

> *On Tizen‑OS 3.x and newer* the setting is usually under **Settings → General → Smart Hub** (or *Home Screen*).  
> *On WebOS* it lives in **Settings → System → Home**.

Once you’ve flipped that switch, the banner tiles disappear. The next time you hit *Home*, you’ll see only your favorite apps and lists.

---

## 2️⃣ If there is no “Show ads” toggle

| Step | What to press / choose |
|-------|------------------------|
| **1.** Press **Apps** (or *My Smart‑TV*) on the Home screen. |
| **2.** Find the app called **“Smart Hub”** or **“Home Screen.”** |
| **3.** Tap its gear icon → **Settings** → look for an **Ad Personalization** switch and turn it OFF. |  

> Some older models hide this inside the *My Smart‑TV* app instead of Settings.

---

## 3️⃣ If you can’t find any toggle at all

| Option | What to do |
|--------|------------|
| **A.** Upgrade your TV’s firmware – newer releases often expose an ad‑toggle. |
| **B.** Disable the whole Smart‑Hub / Home Screen feature: <br>Settings → System → Home → *Turn off “Home screen”* (you’ll lose the default launcher, but ads won’t appear). |
| **C.** Install a third‑party home‑screen app that doesn’t show ads – e.g. “Samsung TV Launcher”, “Kiosk”, or “My Smart TV.” |  
> Note: only some WebOS models allow installing new launchers via the Samsung Store. |

---

## 4️⃣ Network‑level ad blocker (last resort)

If you still see occasional promotional tiles that come from external servers, you can block them on your router:

| Step | What to press / choose |
|-------|------------------------|
| **1.** Install a Pi‑hole or similar DNS‑based ad‑blocker on your network. |
| **2.** Add Samsung’s ad domains (e.g. `*.samsung.com`, `ad.samsung.com`) to the block list. |

> This stops *all* traffic to those servers, so you’ll never see any Samsung‑ads on any device.

---

### TL;DR

1. Press Home → Settings → General/System → Smart Hub/Home Screen → Turn off **Show ads** (or **Ad Personalization**).  
2. If that setting isn’t exist, try turning off the whole Home screen or installing a third‑party launcher.  
3. As a last resort, block Samsung’s ad domains on your router.

That should silence those pop‑up ads on most Samsung Smart‑TVs. Happy viewing!
